# 2. Admission Overview

## Brief Overview

The American University of Phnom Penh (AUPP) offers rolling admission for undergraduate programs. Students may apply throughout the year until available seats are filled. Admission is based on academic qualifications, English proficiency, supporting documents, and, where necessary, interviews or placement assessments.

## Admission Objectives

- Admit academically qualified students.
- Ensure applicants are prepared for English-medium instruction.
- Evaluate applicants using a holistic admission process.
- Support both Cambodian and international students.

## Available Intake(s)

- Fall Intake
- Spring Intake
- Summer Intake (selected programs when available)

## Study Level

- Bachelor's Degree

# 3. Eligibility Requirements

## Educational Qualification

Applicants must have completed one of the following:

- Cambodian Grade 12 (Baccalaureate II)
- International High School Diploma
- A-Level
- IB Diploma
- GED (or equivalent recognized qualification)
- Other equivalent secondary education recognized by AUPP

## National Examination Requirement

- Cambodian students should have successfully completed Grade 12 (Bac II).
- No separate national university entrance examination is required.

## Minimum Academic Requirements

Applicants should demonstrate satisfactory academic performance in high school.

The university evaluates:

- High school GPA
- Academic transcript
- Overall academic achievement

AUPP does not publicly specify a minimum GPA requirement.

## Language Requirements

Since all programs are taught in English, applicants must demonstrate English proficiency through one of the following:

- TOEFL
- IELTS
- Duolingo English Test
- Pearson PTE
- SAT/ACT English evidence (when accepted)
- AUPP English Placement Test
- Previous education conducted in English (subject to approval)

Students who do not meet the English requirement may be placed into the English Foundation Program before beginning degree coursework.

## Age Restrictions

- No published age restriction.

## Citizenship Requirements

- Cambodian citizens are eligible.
- International applicants are eligible.
- Foreign students must satisfy Cambodian visa and immigration requirements.

# 4. Required Documents

Applicants are generally required to submit:

- Completed application form
- High school diploma or graduation certificate
- Official academic transcript
- National ID (Cambodian students)
- Passport (international students)
- Passport-sized photographs
- Proof of English proficiency (if available)
- Personal statement or admission essay (if requested)
- Recommendation letter(s) (if required by program)
- Transfer transcript (transfer applicants)
- Course descriptions/syllabi (transfer credit evaluation)
- Additional supporting documents requested by the Admission Office

International applicants may additionally require:

- Student visa documentation
- Passport copy
- Certified English translations of academic documents (if applicable)

# 5. Admission Process

## Step 1

Pay the application fee (if applicable).

Possible outcomes include:

- Full Admission
- Conditional Admission

## Step 8

Accept admission offer.

## Step 9

Pay tuition deposit and registration fees.

Complete enrollment and course registration.

# 7. Important Dates

AUPP follows **rolling admission**, therefore applications are accepted throughout the year while seats remain available.

Typical admission timeline:

- Application opens: Rolling
- Application deadline: Rolling until intake capacity is reached
- Document submission: Before admission review
- English placement test: Scheduled individually when required
- Admission decision: After document evaluation
- Enrollment: Before semester begins
- Semester start:
  - Fall
  - Spring
  - Summer (where available)

No official fixed annual admission calendar is currently published.
