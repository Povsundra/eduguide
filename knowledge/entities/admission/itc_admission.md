# 2. Admission Overview

## Brief Overview

The Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC) admits students into Engineering and Technician programs.

Admission differs by program:

- **Engineering Program:** Applicants must pass the national Bac II examination and successfully complete the ITC entrance examination.
- **Technician Program:** Admission is based on document screening without an entrance examination.
- **International Engineering Program:** Applicants must satisfy both English proficiency requirements and the ITC entrance examination.

Engineering students begin with a common **Foundation Year (Tronc Commun)** before entering their specialized engineering departments.

## Admission Objectives

- Recruit qualified students with strong foundations in science and mathematics.
- Evaluate applicants through a competitive entrance examination.
- Prepare students for engineering education through the Foundation Year.

## Available Intake(s)

- One intake per academic year.

## Study Level

- Engineering Degree (5-year Engineer Program)
- Technician Diploma

# 3. Eligibility Requirements

## Educational Qualification

Applicants must have:

- Cambodian Bac II Certificate, or
- Equivalent secondary education recognized by the Ministry of Education.

## National Examination Requirement

Applicants must:

- Pass the Cambodian Grade 12 National Examination (Bac II).

## Minimum Academic Requirements

### Engineering Program

Applicants must:

- Pass Bac II.
- Obtain Grade **A–E**.
- Register for the ITC entrance examination.

### Technician Program

Applicants must:

- Pass Bac II.

Admission is based on document evaluation.

## Language Requirements

### National Engineering Program

- No university-wide English requirement.

### International Engineering Program

Applicants must provide one of the following:

- IELTS ≥ 4.5
- TOEFL iBT ≥ 60
- Equivalent English proficiency certificate

## Age Restrictions

- No published age restriction.

## Citizenship Requirements

- Cambodian students are eligible.
- International students may apply through the International Engineering Program.

# 4. Required Documents

Applicants generally submit:

- Completed application form
- Bac II Certificate (or equivalent)
- Academic transcript (if requested)
- National ID card or Passport
- Passport-sized photographs
- Entrance examination registration receipt (Engineering Program)
- English proficiency certificate (International Program only)

Additional documents may be requested during admission.

# 5. Admission Process

## Engineering Program

1. Pass the Cambodian Bac II examination.
2. Register for the ITC entrance examination.
3. Submit required documents.
4. Pay the entrance examination registration fee.
5. Take the entrance examination.
6. Wait for admission results.
7. Complete enrollment.
8. Begin Foundation Year (Tronc Commun).

## Technician Program

1. Pass Bac II.
2. Submit application documents.
3. Document screening.
4. Receive admission result.
5. Complete enrollment.

## International Engineering Program

1. Submit application.
2. Provide English proficiency certificate.
3. Pay entrance examination registration fee.
4. Take the entrance examination.
5. Receive admission decision.
6. Pay tuition.
7. Begin Foundation Year.

# 6. Entrance Examination

## Is Entrance Exam Required?

### Engineering Program

- Yes.

### Technician Program

- No.

### International Engineering Program

- Yes.

## Subjects Tested

Engineering entrance examination covers:

- Mathematics
- Physics
- Chemistry
- Logic

For International Engineering Program, the examination is conducted in English.

## Exam Format

- Written examination
- On-site examination

## Passing Criteria

The official passing score is not publicly published.

Admission is based on examination performance and available capacity.

# 8. Fees

## National Engineering Program

### Entrance Examination Fee

- Published in the annual admission announcement.

### Tuition Fee (2025–2026)

Engineering Program

- Male students: **USD 800/year**
- Female students: **USD 650/year**

Technician Program

- Male students: **USD 350/year**
- Female students: **USD 250/year**

Scholarship exemptions are available for selected first-year students.

---

## International Engineering Program

Application / Entrance Examination Registration Fee

- **USD 30**

Tuition Fee

- **USD 2,500/year**

# 9. Special Admission

## International Students

Applicants must:

- Hold an equivalent high school qualification.
- Meet English language requirements.
- Pass the ITC entrance examination.

---

## Technician Program

Separate admission process.

No entrance examination.

Selection is based on submitted academic documents.

---

## Scholarship Students

ITC provides tuition fee exemptions for a number of first-year Engineering and Technician students according to the annual scholarship allocation and Ministry of Education regulations.

---

## Transfer Students

Transfer admission is not generally published.

Applicants should contact the Registrar or Admission Office for case-by-case evaluation.
