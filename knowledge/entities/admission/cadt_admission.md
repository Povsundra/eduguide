# 2. Admission Overview

## Brief Overview

The Cambodia Academy of Digital Technology (CADT) admits students into undergraduate degree programs through both scholarship and fee-paying pathways. Applicants must satisfy the university's admission requirements and complete the admission procedures established by CADT.

Most undergraduate applicants are required to have completed Cambodian Grade 12 (Baccalaureate II) or an equivalent qualification. Depending on the admission pathway or program, applicants may also be required to participate in entrance examinations, interviews, or other assessments announced by the Academy.

## Admission Objectives

- Recruit qualified students with strong academic potential.
- Support Cambodia's digital workforce development.
- Select applicants capable of succeeding in technology-focused education.

## Available Intake(s)

- One main intake each academic year.
- Additional intakes may be announced when available.

## Study Level

- Bachelor

# 3. Eligibility Requirements

## Educational Qualification

Applicants must possess one of the following:

- Cambodian Grade 12 Certificate (Baccalaureate II)
- Equivalent secondary education recognized by the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport (MoEYS)

## National Examination Requirement

- Completion of the Cambodian Grade 12 National Examination (Bac II) or an equivalent qualification.

## Minimum Academic Requirements

Applicants must satisfy the admission requirements established by CADT.

**Important:**

Some academic programs or scholarship schemes may have additional requirements, including:

- Minimum academic performance.
- Subject-specific background.
- Entrance examination.
- Interview.

These requirements may vary by admission pathway or program.

## Language Requirements

- No university-wide English proficiency requirement is published.
- Some programs delivered in English may require English proficiency or placement assessment.

## Age Restrictions

- No published age restriction.

## Citizenship Requirements

- Cambodian citizens are eligible.
- International students may apply under CADT's international admission process.

# 5. Admission Process

1. Complete the admission application.
2. Submit all required supporting documents.
3. Pay the application fee (if applicable).
4. Admission Office verifies eligibility and submitted documents.
5. Complete entrance examination or interview (if required).
6. Receive admission decision.
7. Confirm acceptance.
8. Complete enrollment and student registration.
9. Begin academic study.
