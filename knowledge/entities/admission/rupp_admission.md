# 2. Admission Overview

## Brief Overview

The Royal University of Phnom Penh admits undergraduate students through two primary pathways:

- **Scholarship (Non Fee-Paying):** Admission is coordinated through the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport (MoEYS). Applicants are selected mainly based on the Cambodian Grade 12 National Examination (Bac II) results and the admission quotas assigned to each department.
- **Fee-Paying (Private):** Applicants apply directly to RUPP for programs that accept fee-paying students.

Most undergraduate programs do **not** require a university-wide entrance examination. However, some specialized programs (such as those offered by the Institute of Foreign Languages (IFL) and Media Management) require additional entrance examinations or interviews.

## Admission Objectives

- Provide higher education opportunities for qualified high school graduates.
- Admit students through both scholarship and fee-paying pathways.
- Ensure students meet the academic requirements established by the university and the Ministry of Education.

## Available Intake(s)

- Annual intake (following the national Grade 12 examination)

## Study Level

- Bachelor

# 3. Eligibility Requirements

## Educational Qualification

Applicants must possess one of the following:

- Cambodian High School Diploma (Bac II)
- Equivalent secondary education recognized by the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport (MoEYS)

## National Examination Requirement

- Completion of the Cambodian Grade 12 National Examination (Bac II).

## Minimum Academic Requirements

Applicants must satisfy the admission requirements established by RUPP and/or MoEYS.

**Important:**

Some academic programs have additional admission requirements, including:

- Minimum grades in specific Grade 12 subjects.
- Department-specific entrance examinations.
- Interviews.
- English language assessments.

These requirements vary by department and should be collected separately under each Program Admission entity.

## Language Requirements

- No university-wide English language requirement.
- Language-related programs may require additional English or foreign language assessments.

## Age Restrictions

- No published age restriction.

## Citizenship Requirements

- Cambodian students are eligible for scholarship and fee-paying admission.
- International applicants may apply through the university's international admission process.

# 4. Required Documents

General undergraduate applicants are required to submit:

- Completed application form
- Photocopy of High School Certificate (Bac II) or equivalent
- Two passport-size photographs
- Biography Form (2 copies)
- Personal Memorandum Form

Additional documents may be requested by individual departments or for international applicants.

---

# 5. Admission Process

## Scholarship Students

1. Complete the Grade 12 National Examination (Bac II).
2. Check the MoEYS scholarship admission announcement.
3. Register at the RUPP Studies Office.
4. Submit all required admission documents.
5. Select the desired degree program (subject to eligibility and available quota).
6. Complete any department-specific entrance examination or interview (if required).
7. Receive admission result.
8. Complete enrollment and student registration.

## Fee-Paying Students

1. Apply directly to RUPP.
2. Submit required documents.
3. Pay required application fees.
4. Complete additional departmental assessments (if required).
5. Receive admission decision.
6. Complete enrollment and registration.

# 7. Important Dates

Admission generally follows the national MoEYS admission schedule.

Typical timeline:

- Grade 12 (Bac II) examination results announced
- Scholarship admission announcement
- Registration period
- Department entrance examinations (where applicable)
- Admission result announcement
- Student enrollment
- First semester begins

**Note:** RUPP does not publish one unified admission calendar for all departments. Dates may vary depending on the admission pathway and program.

# 8. Fees

## Application Fee

- 2,000 Riel (Application Form)

## Examination Fee

- 2,000 Riel (Receipt for Examination)

## Enrollment Fee

- 20,000 Riel per academic year (continuing students; scholarship students also pay the annual registration fee)

## Registration Fee

- Included according to the university registration procedure.

Program tuition varies depending on whether the student is scholarship or fee-paying.

# 9. Special Admission

## International Students

International applicants may apply through RUPP's international admission process.

Additional requirements may include:

- Passport
- Student visa
- Certified academic documents

## Transfer Students

Transfer admission is available according to university regulations.

Applicants should contact the Studies Office or relevant faculty for credit transfer evaluation.

## Scholarship Applicants

Scholarship admission is coordinated by MoEYS.

Selection depends on:

- Grade 12 (Bac II) examination results.
- Department quota.
- Program-specific academic requirements.

## Returning Students

Returning students who suspend their studies for approved reasons must obtain permission from the Department of Higher Education before re-enrollment.
