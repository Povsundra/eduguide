# 2. Admission Overview

## Brief Overview

The National University of Management (NUM) offers undergraduate admission through regular, scholarship, transfer, and international admission pathways. Applicants who have completed Cambodian Grade 12 (Baccalaureate II) or an equivalent qualification may apply for undergraduate programs.

Most undergraduate programs admit students through document verification and university registration. However, **some faculties or schools may require additional entrance examinations, interviews, or English assessments**, depending on the program.

## Admission Objectives

- Admit qualified students into undergraduate programs.
- Support academic excellence and professional development.
- Provide admission opportunities for Cambodian and international students.

## Available Intake(s)

- Main annual undergraduate intake
- Additional intake(s) when announced by the university

## Study Level

- Bachelor

# 3. Eligibility Requirements

## Educational Qualification

Applicants must possess one of the following:

- Cambodian Grade 12 Certificate (Baccalaureate II)
- Equivalent secondary education recognized by the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport (MoEYS)

## National Examination Requirement

- Completion of the Cambodian Grade 12 National Examination (Bac II) or an equivalent qualification.

## Minimum Academic Requirements

Applicants must satisfy the admission requirements established by NUM.

**Important:**

Some faculties or schools may require additional admission criteria, including:

- Entrance examination
- Interview
- English proficiency assessment
- Program-specific academic qualifications

These requirements vary by faculty or program.

## Language Requirements

- No university-wide English language requirement for all undergraduate programs.
- English-medium programs may require English proficiency or placement assessment.

## Age Restrictions

- No published age restriction.

## Citizenship Requirements

- Cambodian citizens are eligible.
- International applicants are eligible under NUM's international admission procedures.

# 4. Required Documents

General undergraduate applicants are typically required to submit:

- Completed application form
- High School Diploma (Bac II) or equivalent
- Academic transcript
- Cambodian National ID Card or Passport
- Passport-sized photographs

Depending on the program, additional documents may include:

- Curriculum Vitae (CV)
- English proficiency certificate (if applicable)
- Personal profile
- Other supporting documents requested by the faculty

International applicants may additionally submit:

- Passport
- Certified translated academic documents
- Student visa documentation (after admission)

---

# 5. Admission Process

The general undergraduate admission process consists of:

1. Select the desired undergraduate program.
2. Complete the application form.
3. Submit all required supporting documents.
4. Admission Office verifies eligibility.
5. Complete any required entrance examination or interview (if applicable).
6. Receive admission decision.
7. Accept the admission offer.
8. Pay tuition and registration fees.
9. Complete enrollment and student registration.

# 6. Entrance Examination

## Is Entrance Exam Required?

**Depends on the faculty or program.**

For most undergraduate programs, a university-wide entrance examination is **not** published.

However, some faculties or schools require:

- Entrance examination
- Interview
- English placement assessment

For example, the NUM School of Public Policy (SPP) requires:

- English entrance examination
- Interview

The NUM Institute of Law (iLAW) also requires:

- English grammar test
- Reading
- Writing
- Interview

## Subjects Tested

Program-dependent.

Examples include:

- English Grammar
- Reading
- Writing
- General Interview

## Exam Format

May include:

- Written examination
- Multiple-choice examination
- Interview

## Passing Criteria

Determined by the respective faculty.

Not published as a university-wide standard.
