# Career Entity: Structural Engineer

## 1. Basic Information

- **Career name:** Structural Engineer
- **Khmer name:** វិស្វកររចនាសម្ព័ន្ធ (Visavakâr Rôchâna Sâmpoăn)
- **Career category:** Engineering / Construction & Infrastructure
- **Industry:** Construction, Real Estate Development, Heavy Industrial (oil & gas, power generation, manufacturing), International Development
- **Career level:** Entry to Senior (many specialized roles expect significant prior experience — 8+ years for senior design/safety-assessment roles)
- **Alternative job titles:** Structural Design Engineer, Structural Engineering Consultant, Structural Safety Assessor, Building Design Engineer

---

## 2. Career Overview

A Structural Engineer specializes within civil/building engineering, focusing specifically on the load-bearing framework of buildings and structures — ensuring they can safely withstand gravity, wind, seismic forces, and other loads. The role centers on structural analysis, calculation, and design (reinforced concrete, steel, masonry, and foundations), distinct from the broader civil engineering scope of roads, water systems, and general infrastructure.

- **Main purpose:** Design and verify that buildings and structures are safe, stable, and code-compliant, from concept design through construction supervision.
- **Importance:** As Cambodia's real estate and construction sector grows — including high-rise residential and hotel projects — structural engineers ensure new buildings meet safety standards and can withstand the loads and conditions they will face over their lifespan.
- **Typical role within an organization:** Works within a construction/engineering consultancy, contractor, or specialized structural design firm; collaborates closely with architects, MEP (mechanical/electrical/plumbing) engineers, project managers, and contractors, and may also work as a technical consultant on development-sector infrastructure and safety-assessment projects.

---

## 3. Responsibilities

- Perform structural calculations and analysis for buildings and structures
- Design structural elements: reinforced concrete, structural steel, masonry, and foundations
- Produce structural analysis and modeling using specialized software (e.g., STAAD, RISA 3D)
- Prepare construction drawings, shop drawings, and as-built drawings
- Review and quality-check drawings and calculations produced by other engineers
- Liaise with architects, other engineering disciplines, and clients to resolve design conflicts
- Conduct site inspections to verify construction matches design and safety requirements
- Assess structural safety of existing buildings and recommend repairs where needed
- Ensure designs comply with applicable local building codes and relevant international standards
- Prepare bills of quantities and technical reports for project teams and clients

**Typical decision-making responsibilities:** Choosing structural systems and materials appropriate to a project's loads and site conditions, deciding whether a structure meets safety standards, recommending repair or reinforcement plans for structural deficiencies, and resolving design conflicts between structural requirements and architectural or MEP design.

## 4. Required Skills

### Technical Skills
- Structural analysis and design (reinforced concrete, steel, masonry, foundations)
- Structural analysis/design software (e.g., STAAD, RISA 3D, or equivalent)
- CAD software (AutoCAD, Revit, or similar) for drawings and modeling
- Knowledge of local and international building codes and safety standards (e.g., Eurocode)
- Site inspection and structural safety assessment
- Bills of quantities and technical documentation preparation
- Building Information Modeling (BIM), increasingly expected on larger projects

### Soft Skills
- Attention to detail (safety-critical calculations)
- Analytical and risk-assessment skills
- Communication (liaising with architects, contractors, and clients)
- Problem solving
- Project coordination
- Professional written reporting (especially for consulting/advisory roles)

---

## 5. Work Environment

**Typical workplaces:**
- Construction and structural engineering consultancy firms
- Real estate developers and general contractors
- Heavy industrial sectors (oil & gas, power generation, manufacturing, refineries) for asset-integrity/inspection roles
- International development organizations and donor-funded programs (e.g., building-safety and economic-development projects)

**Work setting:**
- Mix of office-based design/calculation work and on-site inspection work
- Site visits and provincial travel are common, particularly for safety-assessment and inspection-focused roles
- Physical/outdoor conditions apply when inspecting active construction sites or existing structures
- Consulting roles may involve project-based contracts with defined durations rather than permanent employment

## 7. Job Outlook

- **Current demand:** Present and specialized, with postings ranging from private construction/design firms (residential, hotel, and commercial building projects in Phnom Penh) to donor-funded development programs hiring senior structural engineering consultants for building-safety assessments across Cambodia.
- **Future demand:** Tied closely to continued growth in Cambodia's construction and real estate sector, particularly high-rise residential and hotel development, as well as ongoing donor and government interest in building-safety standards and green building design.
- **Emerging technologies:** Building Information Modeling (BIM) and coordinated MEP/structural design workflows, green/sustainable building design practices, seismic and climate-resilience-focused structural assessment.
- **Industry trends:** Increasing emphasis on applying formal building codes and international standards (such as Eurocode) to Cambodian construction, alongside growing demand for structural safety assessments of existing buildings as part of broader economic development and building-safety programs.
- **Growth opportunities:** A clear specialization path from general civil engineering; experienced structural engineers can move into senior consulting or safety-assessment roles with international development programs, which often offer strong compensation for engineers with 8+ years of experience and local code expertise.

## 9. Cambodia Career Context

- **Common employers in Cambodia:** Construction and structural design firms (e.g., SOMA Construction & Development, 3E Consulting), specialized industrial engineering firms serving heavy industry (e.g., O.E.I Cambodia, serving metals, oil & gas, and power generation clients), and international development contractors delivering donor-funded economic and infrastructure programs (e.g., Cowater International's CAPRED Facility, Australia's bilateral economic development program in Cambodia).
- **Industries hiring this career:** Construction and real estate development, heavy industrial sectors (oil & gas, power, metals), and international development/donor-funded programs.
- **Government demand:** Indirect, primarily through donor-funded programs working alongside government partners on building-safety and economic-resilience initiatives.
- **Private sector demand:** Strong, driven by ongoing high-rise residential, hotel, and commercial construction in Phnom Penh and other urban areas.
- **International organization demand:** Notable — donor-funded development programs specifically recruit senior national structural engineering consultants for building-safety assessments, site inspections, and technical advisory work across Cambodia.
