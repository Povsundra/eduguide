# Career Entity: Cybersecurity Analyst

## 1. Basic Information
- **Career name:** Cybersecurity Analyst
- **Khmer name:** អ្នកវិភាគសន្តិសុខអ៊ីនធឺណិត / សន្តិសុខតិចណូឡូជីព័ត៌មាន (Neak Vipheak Sontesok Internet)
- **Career category:** Information Technology / Security
- **Industry:** Banking & Finance, Telecommunications, Government/Public Sector, International Organizations, IT Services
- **Career level:** Entry to Senior (Junior Analyst → Analyst → Senior Analyst/Incident Responder → Security Manager)
- **Alternative job titles:** Information Security Analyst, Security Analyst, Incident Responder, Security Operations Center (SOC) Analyst, IT Security Officer, Cybersecurity Specialist

## 2. Career Overview
A Cybersecurity Analyst protects an organization's networks, systems, and data from unauthorized access, breaches, and cyberattacks. The main purpose of the role is to monitor for threats, investigate security incidents, and strengthen defenses against evolving cyber risks. In Cambodia, this role has grown in importance as digital banking, e-payments, and government e-services expand, alongside rising regional concern over cyber fraud and scam operations. Within an organization, a Cybersecurity Analyst typically works within an IT or Information Security team, reporting to an IT Manager, Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), or Head of IT.

## 3. Responsibilities
- Monitor networks and systems for suspicious activity and unauthorized access
- Investigate security alerts, incidents, and potential breaches
- Conduct risk assessments and identify system vulnerabilities
- Develop security reports and recommend remediation measures
- Support security audits and ensure compliance with standards (e.g., ISO 27001, ISO 27002)
- Work with IT teams to implement security patches, upgrades, and access controls
- Respond to and help contain cybersecurity incidents (incident response)
- Train staff on cybersecurity awareness (e.g., phishing prevention)
- Stay current on emerging cyber threats, tools, and attack techniques

## 4. Required Skills

### Technical Skills
- Network security and network protocols
- Risk assessment and vulnerability management
- Incident response and digital forensics fundamentals
- Security monitoring tools (e.g., SIEM — Security Information and Event Management)
- Programming/scripting (e.g., Python, Java, C++) for automation and analysis
- Knowledge of cryptography and security frameworks
- Familiarity with compliance standards (ISO 27001/27002)

### Soft Skills
- Analytical and problem-solving skills
- Attention to detail
- Communication (reporting risks clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders)
- Ability to work under pressure during incidents
- Continuous learning mindset
- Genuine interest in protecting digital systems (valued by Cambodian employers)

## 5. Work Environment
- **Common workplaces:** Banks and financial institutions, telecommunications companies, government ministries/agencies, international organizations, IT service and consulting firms, schools/institutions with IT infrastructure
- **Office work:** Standard, often within a Security Operations Center (SOC) or IT department
- **Remote work:** Increasingly available for some monitoring/analysis functions, though many roles remain on-site
- **Field work:** Limited; occasional on-site incident response or infrastructure work
- **Laboratory work:** Not typical, though some roles use dedicated "cyber range" training/testing environments

## 7. Job Outlook
- Cambodia's digital economy continues to expand rapidly (digital banking, e-wallets, e-government services), driving demand for cybersecurity roles across sectors.
- International organizations operating in Cambodia, including UN agencies and development banks, actively recruit cybersecurity analysts and information security officers to secure digital infrastructure and development programs.
- Cambodia passed the Law on Anti-Technology Fraud in April 2026, introducing strict penalties for cyber fraud and scam operations, reflecting heightened national and regional priority on cybersecurity and online fraud prevention.
- A national Personal Data Protection Law has been drafted (following the earlier cybersecurity law framework) but had not yet been enacted as of early 2026, signaling continued regulatory development in this space.
- Local training and certification bodies (e.g., National Institute of Posts, Telecoms & ICT, the Cambodia Cybersecurity Federation, CamTech Cybersecurity Training Center) are expanding capacity-building programs, indicating growing institutional investment in the field.
- In-demand certifications include CompTIA Security+, CISSP, CEH, and SANS GIAC.
