# Career Entity: Biomedical Engineer

## 1. Basic Information

- **Career name:** Biomedical Engineer
- **Khmer name:** វិស្វករជីវេវេជ្ជសាស្ត្រ (Visavakâr Chivâ-Vetchéasastr)
- **Career category:** Engineering / Healthcare Technology
- **Industry:** Healthcare (public and private hospitals), International Development/Health Programs, Medical Equipment Companies
- **Career level:** Entry to Senior
- **Alternative job titles:** Biomedical Equipment Engineer, Biomedical Equipment Technical Officer, Biomedical Equipment Technician (BMET), Medical Equipment Engineer

---

## 2. Career Overview

A Biomedical Engineer applies engineering principles to healthcare, ensuring that medical equipment and devices — from diagnostic machines to therapeutic and life-support instrumentation — are installed, calibrated, maintained, and repaired safely and reliably. The role bridges engineering and clinical practice, keeping the technology that hospitals depend on functioning correctly to protect both patients and healthcare staff.

- **Main purpose:** Ensure the safe, accurate, and reliable operation of medical equipment within healthcare facilities, supporting diagnosis, treatment, and patient safety.
- **Importance:** This is a newly formalizing but increasingly recognized profession in Cambodia — <cite index="138-1">following the signing of a Sub-Decree by the Prime Minister, biomedical positions have been officially recognized within the national public health system for the first time, formally establishing two defined roles: Biomedical Equipment Technical Officer (Associate degree) and Biomedical Equipment Engineer (Bachelor's degree)</cite>, marking a major institutional milestone for the profession in the country.
- **Typical role within an organization:** Works within a hospital's biomedical/technical maintenance department, an international health program, or a medical equipment supplier; coordinates closely with clinical staff, hospital administration, and equipment manufacturers or vendors.

---

## 3. Responsibilities

- Install, inspect, test, and calibrate diagnostic, therapeutic, and life-support medical equipment
- Perform preventive and corrective maintenance on medical devices
- Diagnose and repair malfunctioning equipment
- Ensure equipment complies with safety standards to protect patients and staff
- Maintain technical documentation, inspection records, and maintenance logs
- Manage equipment lifecycle planning and budgeting
- Provide training or guidance to clinical staff on proper equipment use
- Coordinate with hospital administration on equipment procurement and planning
- Support needs assessments and technical specifications for new medical equipment (in program/development roles)
- Travel to different hospital or program sites for equipment servicing and support

**Typical decision-making responsibilities:** Determining whether equipment is safe to remain in use or must be taken offline for repair, prioritizing maintenance and repair work across multiple pieces of equipment or facilities, and recommending equipment replacement versus repair based on lifecycle and cost considerations.

## 4. Required Skills

### Technical Skills
- Electronics and instrumentation fundamentals
- Medical equipment installation, calibration, and repair
- Preventive and corrective maintenance procedures
- Understanding of diagnostic, therapeutic, and life-support device types
- Technical documentation and record-keeping
- Equipment lifecycle and budget management (for senior/lead roles)
- Basic knowledge of relevant safety and quality standards for medical equipment

### Soft Skills
- Attention to detail (patient safety depends on equipment accuracy)
- Problem solving
- Communication (working with clinical staff who are not technically trained)
- Reliability and responsibility
- Willingness to travel, including to rural or provincial sites
- Adaptability (working across varied hospital settings and equipment types)

## 5. Work Environment

**Typical workplaces:**
- Public referral hospitals (of varying size/capacity levels)
- Private hospitals and clinics
- International development/health organizations supporting Cambodia's health system
- Medical equipment supply and servicing companies

**Work setting:**
- Hospital-based, hands-on technical work, often within a dedicated biomedical/maintenance workshop
- Regular travel to program or hospital sites is common, including some travel to provincial areas, occasionally requiring overnight trips
- Combination of workshop-based repair work and on-site equipment servicing within clinical environments
- Physical, hands-on troubleshooting work with electronic and mechanical medical devices

## 7. Job Outlook

- **Current demand:** Emerging but significant — <cite index="140-1">Cambodia has historically lacked enough educated biomedical engineering staff to bring the profession into general practice at every hospital, with a recognized need for qualified technicians to maintain and ensure the safe condition of medical equipment across the healthcare system</cite>. Hospital capacity planning documents specify staffing needs of <cite index="139-1">between 2 and 7 biomedical technicians per referral hospital depending on hospital size (measured in beds)</cite>, indicating substantial unmet demand as facilities scale up.
- **Future demand:** Strong growth expected, driven by the 2024 Sub-Decree formally establishing biomedical positions within the national public health structure, new dedicated training infrastructure (e.g., a new Biomedical Engineering Laboratory at the University of Health Sciences in Phnom Penh, opened November 2024), and ongoing international development support for the profession.
- **Emerging technologies:** Increasing sophistication of diagnostic and therapeutic medical devices, growth of dedicated biomedical engineering degree programs in Cambodia, structured Medical Equipment (ME) Management Systems being introduced at public health facilities.
- **Industry trends:** International development partners (e.g., the Czech Development Agency, EWH capacity-building programs) have been actively supporting the formalization and training of biomedical engineering capacity in Cambodia, alongside government recognition of the profession as an official public health system role.
- **Growth opportunities:** As one of Cambodia's most recently formalized professions with official government recognition, early entrants have significant opportunity for career growth as the profession scales; international health/development organizations (e.g., UN agencies) also recruit senior biomedical engineers for regional technical support roles.

## 9. Cambodia Career Context

- **Common employers in Cambodia:** Public referral hospitals (including major facilities such as Calmette Hospital in Phnom Penh), international development organizations supporting Cambodia's health system (e.g., Assist International, employing Biomedical Equipment Technicians who travel to program hospital sites including provincial locations such as Kampot), and UN agencies (e.g., UNOPS, recruiting senior biomedical engineers for regional health infrastructure support work covering Cambodia and other Asia-Pacific countries).
- **Industries hiring this career:** Public and private healthcare, international health/development programs, medical equipment supply and servicing.
- **Government demand:** Newly formalized and significant — the Cambodian government has, via Sub-Decree, officially established Biomedical Equipment Technical Officer and Biomedical Equipment Engineer as recognized positions within the national public health system.
- **Private sector demand:** Present through private hospitals, clinics, and medical equipment/technology companies, though smaller in scale than the public health system's needs.
- **International organization demand:** Significant — international development agencies and UN bodies have played a leading role in building biomedical engineering capacity in Cambodia, including funding training laboratories, technician training programs, and directly employing biomedical engineers and technicians to support Cambodia's hospital network.
