# Career Entity: AI Engineer

## 1. Basic Information

- **Career name:** AI Engineer
- **Khmer name:** វិស្វករបញ្ញាសិប្បនិម្មិត (Visavakâr Panhnha Seppanimit)
- **Career category:** Technology / Software Engineering
- **Industry:** Information Technology, Banking & Finance, Telecommunications, E-commerce, Startups, Government digital services
- **Career level:** Entry to Senior (many postings require prior software engineering experience before specializing in AI)
- **Alternative job titles:** Machine Learning Engineer, AI/ML Engineer, MLOps Engineer, Applied AI Engineer, LLM Engineer, AI Solutions Engineer

---

## 2. Career Overview

An AI Engineer designs, builds, deploys, and maintains AI and machine learning systems so they run reliably in real products and business processes — moving models from experimentation into production. The role sits closer to software engineering than to research: it emphasizes building pipelines, integrating models (including large language models) into applications, and monitoring system performance in live environments, rather than only analyzing data or producing one-off insights.

- **Main purpose:** Turn machine learning models and AI capabilities into working, deployed systems that function reliably, scale, and integrate into products or business workflows.
- **Importance:** <cite index="20-1">Cambodia's top employers need engineers who can build, deploy, monitor, and maintain AI systems in production</cite>, since a model that only works in a notebook or research environment has limited business value.
- **Typical role within an organization:** Works within an engineering, product, or data/AI platform team; partners with data scientists (who develop models) and software engineers (who build applications), taking responsibility for the infrastructure, deployment pipeline, and reliability of AI features.

---

## 3. Responsibilities

- Design and build systems that integrate machine learning models or AI/LLM APIs into applications
- Develop and maintain data and model pipelines (training, evaluation, deployment)
- Fine-tune or adapt pre-trained models (including large language models) for specific use cases
- Deploy models to production environments and monitor their performance, latency, and reliability
- Build and maintain MLOps infrastructure (model versioning, CI/CD for ML, monitoring/alerting)
- Optimize models and systems for cost, latency, and scalability
- Collaborate with data scientists to productionize research prototypes
- Collaborate with product managers/business stakeholders to translate requirements into AI features
- Ensure responsible use of AI systems (data privacy, bias/quality checks, guardrails)
- Stay current with new AI tools, frameworks, and techniques (e.g., agentic AI, LLM tooling)

**Typical decision-making responsibilities:** Choosing system architecture and deployment approach, deciding when a model is production-ready, balancing accuracy versus latency/cost trade-offs, and setting monitoring thresholds for model performance degradation.

## 4. Required Skills

### Technical Skills
- Programming (Python primarily; also Java, C++, or Go in some engineering-heavy roles)
- Machine learning frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn)
- Software engineering fundamentals (APIs, version control/Git, testing, system design)
- Cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP) for deployment and scaling
- MLOps tools and practices (model deployment, monitoring, CI/CD pipelines)
- SQL and data pipeline / big data tools
- LLM integration and prompt engineering
- Containerization and deployment tooling (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes) in more senior roles

### Soft Skills
- Problem solving
- Communication (translating technical constraints for product/business teams)
- Collaboration/teamwork across engineering, data science, and product functions
- Adaptability (fast-changing tools and techniques)
- Attention to detail
- Ownership and reliability mindset (production systems require accountability)

- E-commerce and logistics platforms

**Work setting:**

## 7. Job Outlook

- **Current demand:** Rising, with Cambodian job platforms and bootcamps identifying <cite index="21-1">AI/Machine Learning Engineer as one of three high-impact specialized roles that Cambodia's 2026 market has crystallized demand around</cite>.
- **Future demand:** National-level AI adoption is projected to have major economic impact, and a large share of current job tasks are considered automatable, which increases demand for engineers who can build and maintain the systems doing that automation rather than reduces demand for the field itself.
- **Emerging technologies:** Agentic AI, large language model (LLM) integration and fine-tuning, retrieval-augmented generation, MLOps/production AI tooling.
- **Industry trends:** Global remote job platforms show substantially higher posting volume for Machine Learning Engineer and AI Engineer roles compared to narrower AI specialist titles, reflecting strong demand for engineers who can operationalize AI rather than only research it.
- **Growth opportunities:** Access to fully remote international roles is more common in this career than in many other Cambodia-based tech roles, widening opportunities beyond the local job market; local demand is also growing at banks, telecoms, and startups building AI-powered products.
