# Career Entity: Laboratory Scientist

## 1. Basic Information
- **Career name:** Laboratory Scientist
- **Khmer name:** អ្នកវិទ្យាសាស្ត្រមន្ទីរពិសោធន៍ (Neak Vityasastr Monti Pisaoth)
- **Career category:** Science / Health Sciences
- **Industry:** Public Health, Biomedical Research, Diagnostics, Food & Environmental Safety, Pharmaceuticals
- **Career level:** Entry to Senior (Laboratory Technician → Laboratory Scientist → Senior Scientist/Lab Manager)
- **Alternative job titles:** Medical Laboratory Scientist, Clinical Laboratory Scientist, Biomedical Scientist, Medical Biologist, Research Scientist (Lab-based), Laboratory Technician (junior variant)

## 2. Career Overview
A Laboratory Scientist designs and conducts experiments, tests, and analyses on biological, chemical, or environmental samples to generate accurate scientific or diagnostic data. The main purpose of the role is to produce reliable results that support medical diagnosis, disease surveillance, food/water safety, or scientific research. In Cambodia, this role is important for public health institutions and research bodies working on infectious disease, water/food safety, and clinical diagnostics. Within an organization, a Laboratory Scientist typically works under a Lab Manager or Head of Laboratory, often as part of a multidisciplinary research or diagnostic unit.

## 3. Responsibilities
- Collect, prepare, and handle biological, chemical, or environmental samples
- Perform laboratory tests and diagnostic procedures (e.g., microscopy, molecular testing, culture-based methods)
- Operate, calibrate, and maintain laboratory equipment and instruments
- Record, verify, and report test results and experimental data accurately
- Follow standard operating procedures (SOPs), biosafety, and quality control/quality assurance protocols (e.g., Good Laboratory Practice, ISO 15189, ISO 17025)
- Troubleshoot equipment or procedural issues
- Contribute to research protocol design and, in research settings, to scientific publications and reports
- Train and supervise junior lab staff or technicians

## 4. Required Skills

### Technical Skills
- Laboratory techniques (microscopy, sample analysis, molecular/serological testing)
- Instrumentation operation and maintenance
- Quality control and Good Laboratory Practice (GLP)
- Data recording, statistics, and basic data analysis
- Knowledge of biosafety and biosecurity regulations
- Familiarity with laboratory information systems / basic computer software

### Soft Skills
- Attention to detail and precision
- Analytical and problem-solving skills
- Ability to work under deadlines in a fast-paced environment
- Communication (reporting results to clinicians, researchers, or supervisors)
- Adaptability to new methods and technologies
- Teamwork and collaboration within multidisciplinary teams

## 5. Work Environment
- **Common workplaces:** Hospitals and clinics, public health/research institutes, diagnostic laboratories, pharmaceutical and food companies, environmental/water testing labs, universities
- **Laboratory work:** Predominant mode of work
- **Office work:** Moderate — report writing, data review, documentation
- **Field work:** Occasional — sample collection (e.g., water, food, or field epidemiological surveys)
- **Remote work:** Rare; role is inherently hands-on and lab-based

## 7. Job Outlook
- Cambodia's public health and research infrastructure is anchored by institutions such as Institut Pasteur du Cambodge (IPC), which conducts diagnostics, vaccination services, and food/water safety testing, and which regularly recruits laboratory technicians, medical biologists, and laboratory scientists.
- Growing demand exists in infectious disease diagnostics, antimicrobial resistance (AMR) surveillance, and food/environmental safety testing.
- International organizations and embassies in Phnom Penh (e.g., research-linked institutions) also periodically recruit laboratory/medical research specialists.
- Emerging trends include molecular diagnostics, quality accreditation (ISO 15189, ISO 17025), and increased regional collaboration on disease surveillance.
- Career growth is often tied to postgraduate training or international academic partnerships available through institutes in Phnom Penh.

## 9. Cambodia Career Context
- **Common employers:** Institut Pasteur du Cambodge (IPC), public and referral hospitals (e.g., Calmette Hospital), Ministry of Health-affiliated facilities, food/water safety and quality control bodies, international research partners, embassies with health/research programs
- **Industries hiring:** Public health and biomedical research, clinical diagnostics, food and environmental safety, pharmaceuticals
- **Government demand:** Present — Ministry of Health and affiliated public hospitals/labs
- **Private sector demand:** Moderate — private hospitals/clinics and food/agriculture testing companies
- **International organization demand:** Notable — IPC (part of the international Pasteur Network) and foreign embassies/research programs recruit laboratory scientists and technicians, often for public health and infectious disease research roles
