# Career Entity: DevOps Engineer

## 1. Basic Information

- **Career name:** DevOps Engineer
- **Khmer name:** វិស្វករដេវអុបស៍ (Visavakâr DevOps)
- **Career category:** Technology / Infrastructure & Software Engineering
- **Industry:** Information Technology, Banking & Finance, Telecommunications, E-commerce, Cloud/Managed Services
- **Career level:** Entry to Senior (many roles expect prior software engineering or systems administration experience)
- **Alternative job titles:** Site Reliability Engineer (SRE), Cloud Engineer, Infrastructure Engineer, Platform Engineer, Release Engineer

---

## 2. Career Overview

A DevOps Engineer bridges software development and IT operations, building the tools, pipelines, and infrastructure that let software be built, tested, deployed, and monitored quickly and reliably. Rather than writing application features, the role focuses on the systems and automation that support every other engineering team — deployment pipelines, cloud infrastructure, monitoring, and incident response.

- **Main purpose:** Enable fast, reliable, and safe delivery of software by automating deployment, managing infrastructure, and reducing downtime.
- **Importance:** <cite index="79-1">Companies implementing DevOps practices have seen meaningful reductions in system downtime and significantly faster project delivery times</cite>, making this role central to how modern software teams operate at scale.
- **Typical role within an organization:** Works within an engineering or IT infrastructure team, collaborating closely with software developers (to build deployment pipelines), security teams (to enforce practices), and operations/support teams (to manage live systems).

---

## 3. Responsibilities

- Design, build, and maintain CI/CD (continuous integration/continuous deployment) pipelines
- Manage cloud infrastructure (e.g., AWS, Azure, GCP) and on-premises servers
- Automate repetitive operational tasks using scripting and infrastructure-as-code
- Configure and manage containerization and orchestration systems (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes)
- Monitor system performance, uptime, and reliability; set up alerting and logging
- Perform root-cause analysis, troubleshooting, and incident response for production issues
- Implement security best practices across infrastructure and deployment pipelines
- Collaborate with developers to streamline the build-test-deploy workflow
- Manage configuration and environment consistency across development, staging, and production
- Document infrastructure setup, deployment processes, and incident postmortems

**Typical decision-making responsibilities:** Choosing cloud providers, tools, and automation approaches; deciding how to respond to production incidents; balancing system reliability/security against speed of deployment; setting monitoring thresholds and escalation processes.

---

## 4. Required Skills

### Technical Skills
- Linux/Unix systems administration
- Scripting and automation (Bash, Python, or Go)
- Cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud)
- Containerization and orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes)
- CI/CD tools and pipeline design
- Infrastructure-as-code (e.g., Terraform, Ansible)
- Monitoring and logging tools
- Networking and security fundamentals
- Version control (Git)

### Soft Skills
- Problem solving under pressure (incident response)
- Communication (coordinating across development and operations teams)
- Attention to detail
- Collaboration/teamwork
- Adaptability (rapidly evolving tools and cloud services)
- Ownership and reliability mindset

## 5. Work Environment

**Typical workplaces:**
- IT service and software development companies
- Banks and fintech companies
- Telecommunications companies
- E-commerce platforms
- Cloud and managed-services providers
- International companies hiring remote DevOps talent

**Work setting:**
- Primarily office-based or remote, computer-intensive infrastructure work
- On-call rotations are common, since production systems need monitoring and incident response outside standard hours
- Remote and fully-remote international roles are common for this career
- Field work and laboratory work are not part of this role

## 7. Job Outlook

- **Current demand:** Strong. <cite index="79-1">DevOps Engineer is ranked among the top high-paying tech roles in Cambodia, requiring mastery of Python, Go, Bash, Docker, and Kubernetes along with cloud platform experience such as AWS and Azure</cite>.
- **Future demand:** <cite index="79-1">Cambodia's IT services market is projected to reach roughly $350.90 million by 2029, supporting continued demand for DevOps talent</cite>, alongside a broader projected shortage of IT professionals in the country.
- **Emerging technologies:** Platform engineering (building internal self-service developer platforms), AI-assisted infrastructure automation and incident response, expanded use of Kubernetes and cloud-native architectures, MLOps (DevOps practices applied to machine learning systems).
- **Industry trends:** DevOps and its variations (such as MLOps) have become a primary software delivery paradigm, with the role increasingly combining technical automation skills with architectural thinking and cross-team collaboration rather than pure tool operation.
- **Growth opportunities:** DevOps roles are frequently cited among the highest-paying tech positions in Cambodia's local market; strong access to fully remote international roles gives Cambodia-based DevOps engineers a path to significantly higher compensation than typical local-market rates.

*(Reported salary ranges varied dramatically depending on whether the source described local Cambodian hiring or remote international employment — local rankings cite figures reaching roughly $60K, while global remote-salary platforms report considerably higher medians. Because these reflect different labor markets rather than one consistent figure, specific numbers are not stated as a single fact here; treat local and international remote compensation as distinct.)*

## 9. Cambodia Career Context

- **Common employers in Cambodia:** IT service companies, banks and fintech firms, telecommunications companies, and international companies hiring remote DevOps engineers based in Cambodia.
- **Industries hiring this career:** Information technology services, banking and finance, telecommunications, e-commerce.
- **Government demand:** Indirect — supported by the government's "Digital Cambodia" initiative promoting digital infrastructure and IT job creation.
- **Private sector demand:** The dominant source of demand, with DevOps consistently identified as one of the higher-paying local tech roles as more Cambodian companies adopt cloud infrastructure and CI/CD practices.
- **International organization demand:** Present mainly through remote DevOps/infrastructure roles for international companies rather than through local offices of international organizations.
