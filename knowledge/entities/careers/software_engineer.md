# Career Entity: Software Engineer

## 1. Basic Information

- **Career name:** Software Engineer
- **Khmer name:** វិស្វករសូហ្វវែរ (Visavakâr Software)
- **Career category:** Technology / Software Engineering
- **Industry:** Information Technology, Banking & Finance, Telecommunications, E-commerce, Healthcare Tech, Agriculture Tech, Government digital services
- **Career level:** Entry to Senior
- **Alternative job titles:** Software Developer, Web Developer, Mobile App Developer, Backend Engineer, Frontend Engineer, Full-Stack Developer, IT Engineer (used broadly in Cambodia's local job market)

---

## 2. Career Overview

A Software Engineer designs, builds, tests, and maintains software applications and systems — websites, mobile apps, backend services, and business systems — that solve practical problems for organizations and users. The role covers the full lifecycle of software: understanding requirements, writing and reviewing code, testing, deploying, and maintaining/improving systems over time.

- **Main purpose:** Build reliable, functional software that meets user and business needs, from internal business tools to consumer-facing apps and platforms.
- **Importance:** As Cambodian organizations digitize operations — banking, e-commerce, healthcare, agriculture, and government services — <cite index="34-1">software engineers, alongside data scientists and cloud specialists, are in particularly high demand, driven by expected growth in the technology sector and a shortage of experienced personnel</cite>.
- **Typical role within an organization:** Works within a development or IT team, often organized around a specific product, platform, or business system; collaborates with product managers, designers, QA, and (in AI-adjacent teams) data scientists/AI engineers.

---

## 3. Responsibilities

- Write, test, and debug code across one or more programming languages
- Design software architecture for new features or systems
- Build and maintain web applications, mobile applications, or backend services/APIs
- Collaborate with product managers and designers to translate requirements into working software
- Conduct code reviews and follow software engineering best practices
- Write automated tests and participate in QA processes
- Deploy and maintain applications, including basic DevOps tasks (CI/CD, monitoring)
- Troubleshoot and resolve bugs or production issues
- Maintain and refactor existing codebases
- Document code and technical decisions for team collaboration

**Typical decision-making responsibilities:** Choosing appropriate technologies/frameworks for a given problem, structuring code and system architecture, deciding how to fix or refactor problematic code, and estimating time/effort for features.

## 4. Required Skills

### Technical Skills
- Programming languages (e.g., JavaScript/TypeScript, Python, Java, PHP, C#, depending on specialization)
- Web development frameworks (e.g., React, Node.js, Django, Laravel)
- Mobile app development (Android/iOS or cross-platform frameworks) for mobile-focused roles
- Databases and SQL
- Version control (Git)
- API design and integration
- Basic cloud and DevOps knowledge (deployment, CI/CD, containerization)
- Software testing and debugging

### Soft Skills
- Problem solving
- Communication (with teammates and non-technical stakeholders)
- Teamwork and collaboration
- Attention to detail
- Adaptability (new tools, frameworks, and requirements change frequently)
- Time management

## 5. Work Environment

**Typical workplaces:**
- Technology companies and startups
- Banks and fintech companies
- Telecommunications companies
- E-commerce and logistics platforms
- Healthcare technology and agriculture technology organizations
- Government digital-service and technology park initiatives
- International companies hiring remote developers based in Cambodia

**Work setting:**
- Primarily office-based, computer-intensive work
- Remote and hybrid work is common, including <cite index="33-1">Cambodia serving as an attractive base for remote developers working for international companies, supported by its low cost of living and improving digital infrastructure</cite>
- Field work and laboratory work are uncommon; this is a desk-based development role

## 7. Job Outlook

- **Current demand:** Strong and growing. <cite index="30-1">The tech sector in Cambodia employs an estimated 1.2 million people according to the International Labour Organisation, with around 260,000 identified as tech professionals, most working in software engineering, web development, and IT services</cite>.
- **Future demand:** <cite index="30-1">The number of tech workers in Cambodia is expected to double within the next five years</cite>, driven by growth in startups, digital infrastructure, and foreign tech investment.
- **Emerging technologies:** Integration of AI/LLM features into standard software products, mobile-first "super-app" development, cloud-native architecture, fintech and blockchain-based applications.
- **Industry trends:** <cite index="33-1">Strong demand exists for developers skilled in mobile app development for Android and iOS, particularly for super-apps that combine multiple services, alongside a growing fintech and blockchain niche</cite>.
- **Growth opportunities:** Government-backed digitalization programs and technology parks; strong pipeline into fintech, e-commerce, and healthcare/agriculture technology; access to international remote roles is a distinctive growth path for engineers with globally competitive skills.

*(Specific salary figures vary widely across sources — informal job-board estimates versus international remote-work rate cards differ by an order of magnitude — and no single official Cambodian government or professional-association wage source was found; figures are therefore omitted per data-quality guidance.)*

## 9. Cambodia Career Context

- **Common employers in Cambodia:** <cite index="27-1">Major employers such as ABA Bank and Smart Axiata are actively hiring for technology roles</cite>; the broader market includes local startups, IT service companies, e-commerce platforms, and international companies hiring remote developers.
- **Industries hiring this career:** Banking and fintech, telecommunications, e-commerce and logistics, healthcare technology, agriculture technology, and government digital services.
- **Government demand:** Growing, tied to national digitalization programs and technology park initiatives promoted by the Cambodian government.
- **Private sector demand:** The largest source of demand, spanning banks, telecoms, startups, and IT service firms, with Phnom Penh as the center of activity.
- **International organization demand:** Present mainly through remote software engineering roles for international companies, rather than through local offices of international organizations; Cambodia's lower cost of living relative to regional hubs is cited as a factor attracting this remote work.
