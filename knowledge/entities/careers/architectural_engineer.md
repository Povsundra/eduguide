# Career Entity: Architectural Engineer

## 1. Basic Information

- **Career name:** Architectural Engineer
- **Khmer name:** វិស្វករស្ថាបត្យកម្ម (Visavakâr Sthapatyakâm)
- **Career category:** Engineering / Construction & Built Environment
- **Industry:** Construction, Real Estate Development, Retail/Commercial Facilities, Architecture & Engineering Consultancy
- **Career level:** Entry to Senior
- **Alternative job titles:** Architectural Engineer Specialist, Building Engineer, Design & Construction Engineer, Architect-Engineer

---

## 2. Career Overview

An Architectural Engineer works at the intersection of architecture and engineering, applying engineering principles (structural, mechanical, electrical, and construction methods) to the design, construction, and technical execution of buildings — translating architectural design intent into buildable, code-compliant, and technically sound construction. Unlike an Architect (who focuses primarily on design, aesthetics, and spatial planning) or a Structural Engineer (who focuses narrowly on load-bearing structure), an Architectural Engineer bridges design and technical/construction execution across a building's full scope.

- **Main purpose:** Ensure that architectural designs are technically executable — coordinating structural, construction, and building-systems considerations so that a design becomes a safely built, functioning structure.
- **Importance:** As Cambodia's real estate and commercial construction sectors expand, architectural engineers help translate rapid urban development and design ambitions into buildings that are safe, functional, and built to standard — coordinating between architects, structural/MEP engineers, and contractors.
- **Typical role within an organization:** Works within an architecture-engineering firm, real estate developer, construction company, or corporate facilities/asset management team; liaises with architects, structural and MEP engineers, contractors, and vendors throughout design and construction.

---

## 3. Responsibilities

- Apply engineering fundamentals to review and support architectural designs for buildability and code compliance
- Prepare and review architectural/technical drawings using AutoCAD, Revit (BIM), SketchUp, or similar tools
- Coordinate space planning with structural, construction-method, and materials considerations
- Manage project budgeting, scheduling, and tendering processes
- Coordinate with contractors and vendors during construction and fit-out
- Oversee construction, repairs, maintenance, and remodel/renovation projects for buildings or facility portfolios
- Ensure worksite safety requirements are understood and followed
- Liaise between internal teams (project management, asset management) and external consultants, architects, vendors, and constructors
- Support documentation processes for construction and facilities projects
- Assist in resolving design-to-construction conflicts across disciplines

**Typical decision-making responsibilities:** Choosing construction methods and materials appropriate to a design intent and budget, coordinating trade-offs between architectural vision and structural/technical feasibility, and approving vendor/contractor work against design and safety standards.

## 4. Required Skills

### Technical Skills
- Architectural design and space planning principles
- Building construction methods and materials knowledge
- Engineering fundamentals (structural, and often basic MEP awareness)
- AutoCAD (2D & 3D), Revit (BIM), SketchUp, and rendering software
- Project management: budgeting, scheduling, tendering, contractor coordination
- Worksite safety standards and requirements
- Documentation and technical reporting

### Soft Skills
- Analytical and problem-solving skills
- Communication (with architects, engineers, contractors, and vendors)
- Teamwork and cross-disciplinary coordination
- Flexibility and adaptability in dynamic project environments
- Attention to detail
- Project/vendor management

---

## 5. Work Environment

**Typical workplaces:**
- Architecture and engineering (A&E) consultancy firms
- Real estate developers and construction companies
- Corporate facilities/asset management teams (e.g., retail chains managing store construction and maintenance)
- Design studios combining architecture and engineering services

**Work setting:**
- Mix of office-based design/coordination work and on-site construction supervision
- Site visits for construction, repairs, and maintenance oversight are a regular part of this role
- Coordination-heavy role, often involving meetings with multiple external parties (architects, contractors, vendors)
- Physical/outdoor conditions apply when overseeing active construction or fit-out sites
