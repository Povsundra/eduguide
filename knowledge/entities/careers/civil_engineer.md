# Career Entity: Civil Engineer

## 1. Basic Information

- **Career name:** Civil Engineer
- **Khmer name:** វិស្វករសំណង់ស៊ីវិល (Visavakâr Samnâng Civil)
- **Career category:** Engineering / Construction & Infrastructure
- **Industry:** Construction, Real Estate Development, Government Infrastructure, Water Resources, Transportation
- **Career level:** Entry to Senior
- **Alternative job titles:** Structural Engineer, Infrastructure Engineer, Site Engineer, Project Engineer (Civil), Construction Engineer, Irrigation Engineer, Transportation Engineer

---

## 2. Career Overview

A Civil Engineer designs, plans, and oversees the construction and maintenance of physical infrastructure — roads, bridges, buildings, water systems, and irrigation networks — that societies rely on. The role combines technical design work (using engineering principles and software) with on-site supervision to ensure projects are built safely, to specification, and within regulatory requirements.

- **Main purpose:** Plan, design, and oversee the construction of infrastructure that is safe, functional, and durable, from buildings and roads to water and irrigation systems.
- **Importance:** <cite index="102-1">Civil engineers are much sought after in Cambodia given the large number of real estate and infrastructure projects underway, with the government's support of major projects including urban developments, bridges, and roadways increasing demand for competent engineers</cite>.
- **Typical role within an organization:** Works within a construction firm, engineering consultancy, government ministry, or development project unit; collaborates with architects, contractors, project managers, and government regulators, often splitting time between office-based design work and on-site supervision.

---

## 3. Responsibilities

- Design civil infrastructure (roads, bridges, buildings, water/irrigation systems) using engineering principles and CAD software
- Prepare technical drawings, specifications, and construction documents
- Conduct site surveys and feasibility studies
- Perform structural, load, and materials analysis to ensure safety and durability
- Oversee construction activities on-site to ensure work matches designs and specifications
- Ensure compliance with building codes, safety standards, and environmental regulations
- Coordinate with architects, contractors, surveyors, and government regulators
- Manage project schedules, budgets, and resources
- Inspect completed work and address defects or design issues
- Prepare reports and documentation for stakeholders and government approval processes

**Typical decision-making responsibilities:** Choosing appropriate materials, structural designs, and construction methods; deciding how to resolve on-site design or construction issues; approving or rejecting contractor work against specifications; balancing cost, safety, and regulatory compliance in project decisions.

## 4. Required Skills

### Technical Skills
- Structural analysis and design principles
- CAD software (AutoCAD, MicroStation, Revit, or similar)
- Knowledge of construction materials and methods
- Site surveying
- Project scheduling and cost estimation
- Understanding of building codes and regulatory standards
- Water resources / irrigation engineering (for relevant specializations)
- Basic GIS (Geographic Information Systems) familiarity, increasingly useful

### Soft Skills
- Attention to detail
- Problem solving
- Communication (explaining technical factors clearly to non-technical stakeholders)
- Project management
- Team coordination (architects, contractors, regulators)
- Organizational skills

---

## 5. Work Environment

**Typical workplaces:**
- Construction and engineering consultancy firms
- Real estate development companies
- Government ministries (e.g., infrastructure, water resources, public works)
- International development organizations funding infrastructure projects
- Multinational construction firms operating in Cambodia

**Work setting:**
- Mix of office-based design work and on-site fieldwork at construction sites
- Field work is a substantial and regular part of this role, including site inspections, supervision, and travel to project locations, sometimes outside major cities
- Office work involves design, drafting, and project documentation
- Physical/outdoor conditions are common when working on active construction sites

## 7. Job Outlook

- **Current demand:** Strong. <cite index="104-1">Civil engineering jobs are consistently ranked among the top growing job opportunities in Cambodia, with high demand from companies in the construction sector making it one of the most sought-after specializations</cite>.
- **Future demand:** <cite index="102-1">Cambodia's economic expansion, growing real estate and infrastructure investment, and increasing foreign investment continue to drive demand for civil engineers as the country becomes more urbanized</cite>.
- **Emerging technologies:** Building Information Modeling (BIM), GIS-integrated infrastructure planning, increased use of sustainable/climate-resilient construction materials and methods, digital project management tools.
- **Industry trends:** <cite index="102-1">Major government-backed projects such as urban developments, bridges, and roadways continue to expand demand, alongside growth in energy, transportation, and telecommunications infrastructure requiring civil engineering input</cite>.
- **Growth opportunities:** Government ministries and donor-funded infrastructure programs (e.g., water resources and rural development projects) offer a public-sector career path alongside private construction and consultancy roles; multinational construction firms operating in Cambodia offer opportunities for engineers with strong technical and project-management skills.

## 9. Cambodia Career Context

- **Common employers in Cambodia:** Multinational and local construction firms, real estate developers, engineering consultancies, and government ministries such as the Ministry of Economy and Finance and the Ministry of Water Resources and Meteorology (for donor-funded infrastructure and irrigation projects).
- **Industries hiring this career:** Construction, real estate development, government infrastructure and public works, water resources and irrigation, transportation.
- **Government demand:** Significant — government ministries directly hire or engage civil/infrastructure engineers for major public projects, including donor-funded programs (e.g., rural development and water resources projects engaging national infrastructure and irrigation engineer consultants).
- **Private sector demand:** Strong, led by multinational and local construction firms and real estate developers responding to Cambodia's ongoing urban development and real estate growth.
- **International organization demand:** Present through donor-funded infrastructure and development projects (e.g., water resources and rural development programs) that hire national civil/infrastructure engineering consultants, as well as European and other foreign business associations advertising civil engineering roles for infrastructure projects in Cambodia.
