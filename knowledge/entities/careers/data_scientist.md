# Career Entity: Data Scientist

## 1. Basic Information

- **Career name:** Data Scientist
- **Khmer name:** អ្នកវិទ្យាសាស្ត្រទិន្នន័យ (Neak Vityasas Tinnodâmn)
- **Career category:** Technology / Analytics
- **Industry:** Information Technology, Banking & Finance, Telecommunications, Development & NGO sector, Government, E-commerce
- **Career level:** Entry to Senior (role scope expands significantly with experience)
- **Alternative job titles:** Data Analyst (junior/adjacent), Machine Learning Engineer (adjacent, more engineering-focused), Statistical Analyst, Business Intelligence Analyst (adjacent), AI/ML Researcher (senior/specialized), Research Data Analyst

---

## 2. Career Overview

A Data Scientist collects, cleans, analyzes, and models data to help organizations make evidence-based decisions. The role combines statistics, programming, and domain knowledge to identify patterns, build predictive models, and translate technical findings into actionable recommendations for stakeholders.

- **Main purpose:** Turn raw data into insight and predictive capability that supports strategic and operational decisions.
- **Importance:** As organizations in Cambodia digitize records, transactions, and services, the ability to extract reliable insight from data becomes a competitive and operational necessity — for pricing, risk, customer targeting, forecasting, and policy design.
- **Typical role within an organization:** Sits within an analytics, IT, strategy, or research unit; works closely with business/product teams, engineers, and management, translating business questions into data questions and model outputs back into recommendations.

---

## 3. Responsibilities

- Collect and clean data from databases, APIs, surveys, or sensors
- Explore and visualize data to identify patterns and anomalies
- Design and run statistical tests and experiments (e.g., A/B testing)
- Build, validate, and maintain predictive/machine learning models (e.g., forecasting, classification, clustering)
- Build data pipelines (ETL) to automate data collection and processing
- Build dashboards and reports for stakeholders (e.g., Power BI, Tableau)
- Communicate findings and recommendations to non-technical decision-makers
- Monitor model performance in production and retrain/update as needed
- Collaborate with engineers, product managers, and domain experts on data-driven projects
- Ensure data quality, integrity, and appropriate handling of sensitive data

**Typical decision-making responsibilities:** Choosing appropriate methods/models for a business question, deciding when data quality is sufficient for analysis, flagging risk/uncertainty in model outputs, and recommending whether a model is ready for deployment.

## 4. Required Skills

### Technical Skills
- Programming (Python and/or R)
- SQL and database querying
- Statistics and probability
- Machine learning frameworks (e.g., scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch)
- Data visualization tools (Power BI, Tableau, matplotlib/Seaborn)
- Data wrangling / ETL pipeline development
- Big data and cloud platform familiarity (increasingly expected)
- Version control (Git) and basic software engineering practices

### Soft Skills
- Communication (explaining technical results to non-technical audiences)
- Problem solving
- Critical thinking
- Attention to detail
- Curiosity and continuous learning
- Collaboration/teamwork across departments
- Business/domain understanding

## 7. Job Outlook

- **Current demand:** Growing, driven by digitization across banking, telecom, e-commerce, and the development/NGO sector in Cambodia; job postings for data science and analytics roles appear regularly on regional and local job boards.
- **Future demand:** <cite index="2-1">Cambodia's national commitment to AI is projected to add billions to the national GDP, with a large share of existing jobs facing high automation risk</cite> — a trend that increases demand for people who can build and manage data/AI systems rather than perform routine tasks.
- **Emerging technologies:** Machine learning and AI integration into business analytics, generative AI and large language model (LLM) tools, cloud-based data platforms.
- **Industry trends:** <cite index="9-1">Globally, data analytics has consistently ranked as one of the sectors with the highest share of job postings mentioning generative AI</cite>, reflecting a broader trend of AI skills becoming a core expectation for data science roles rather than a specialization.
- **Growth opportunities:** Increasing local demand from banks, telecom operators, logistics/e-commerce firms, and international development organizations operating in Cambodia; growing pool of local training providers signals rising employer demand for these skills.

## 9. Cambodia Career Context

- **Common employers in Cambodia:** Banks and financial institutions (e.g., ABA Bank), telecommunications operators (e.g., Smart Axiata, MetFone, Cellcard), development/research organizations (e.g., <cite index="17-1">Cambodia Development Resource Institute, which hires data scientists to analyze large datasets and support policy-informing research initiatives</cite>), and international development programs (e.g., USAID-funded health and technology-for-development projects operating in Cambodia).
- **Industries hiring this career:** Banking and finance, telecommunications, logistics/e-commerce, development/NGO research, government digital initiatives.
- **Government demand:** Emerging, tied to national digital economy and e-government initiatives.
- **Private sector demand:** Strongest current source of demand, particularly banking, telecom, and technology-oriented private firms.
- **International organization demand:** Present through development and research institutions running data-driven programs (health, agriculture, governance, and utilities/infrastructure projects) in Cambodia.
