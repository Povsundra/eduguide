# Career Entity: Financial Analyst

## 1. Basic Information
- **Career name:** Financial Analyst
- **Khmer name:** អ្នកវិភាគហិរញ្ញវត្ថុ (Neak Vipheak Herunhvattho)
- **Career category:** Finance / Business
- **Industry:** Banking, Microfinance, FMCG/Corporate Finance, Investment, NGOs, International Organizations
- **Career level:** Entry to Senior (progresses from Junior/Analyst to Senior Analyst to Manager)
- **Alternative job titles:** Finance Analyst, Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) Analyst, Investment Analyst, Budget Analyst, Corporate Finance Analyst

## 2. Career Overview
A Financial Analyst evaluates financial data — budgets, forecasts, market trends, and company performance — to support business decision-making. The role's main purpose is to translate raw financial data into actionable insight for management, investors, or clients. In Cambodia, this role is important for helping banks, corporations, and development institutions assess risk, plan budgets, and evaluate investments in a fast-growing but still-developing financial market. Within an organization, a Financial Analyst typically sits within the finance or FP&A department, reporting to a Finance Manager, Controller, or CFO.

## 3. Responsibilities
- Collect, clean, and analyze financial data (revenue, costs, cash flow)
- Build and maintain financial models for forecasting and budgeting
- Prepare monthly/quarterly/annual financial reports and variance analysis
- Support month-end and quarter-end closing activities (journal entries, accruals, reconciliations)
- Evaluate investment opportunities, projects, or partnerships and recommend actions
- Prepare presentations and reports for senior management and external stakeholders
- Monitor financial performance, flag risks, and recommend corrective action
- Study business, market, and economic trends relevant to the organization's sector

## 4. Required Skills

### Technical Skills
- Financial modeling and forecasting
- Budgeting and variance analysis
- Microsoft Excel (advanced) and financial software/ERP systems
- Statistics and data analysis
- SQL / data querying (increasingly expected as finance digitizes)
- Accounting principles and financial statement analysis
- Familiarity with tools like Power BI for reporting dashboards

### Soft Skills
- Communication (written and verbal; English is commonly required, Khmer is an advantage in local firms)
- Analytical thinking and problem-solving
- Attention to detail and accuracy
- Ability to work under tight deadlines with minimal supervision
- Stakeholder/client communication
- Confidentiality and professional judgment

## 5. Work Environment
- **Common workplaces:** Commercial banks, microfinance institutions, corporate finance departments (FMCG, telecom, real estate/hospitality), international organizations, NGOs, government/regulatory bodies
- **Office work:** Standard for most positions (predominant mode)
- **Remote work:** Uncommon but growing slightly (some international/remote-friendly firms hire Cambodia-based analysts)
- **Field work:** Limited; occasional site visits for project/investment evaluation
- **Laboratory work:** Not applicable

## 7. Job Outlook
- Cambodia's banking and financial sector continues to expand: outstanding loans reached roughly $63 billion and deposits about $65.7 billion by end-2025, with total system assets around $101.8 billion, indicating an active, growing employer base for financial analysis roles.
- Digital finance is accelerating (e.g., Bakong payment system, e-wallets), increasing demand for analysts comfortable with financial technology and data tools.
- Non-performing loans have risen to notable levels in recent years, increasing demand for analysts skilled in credit risk and portfolio analysis.
- Growth sectors generating financial analyst demand include banking, microfinance, real estate/hospitality, construction, telecom, and FMCG.
- Emerging skill demand: financial technology, data analytics, AI-assisted forecasting, and ESG/sustainability-linked finance reporting.

## 9. Cambodia Career Context
- **Common employers:** ABA Bank, Wing Bank, ACLEDA Bank, Canadia Bank, microfinance institutions, FMCG companies, real estate/hospitality groups, international/multinational companies operating in Phnom Penh
- **Industries hiring:** Banking and microfinance, fintech, telecommunications, real estate and construction, FMCG, NGOs and development organizations
- **Government demand:** Moderate — regulatory and public financial management bodies (e.g., National Bank of Cambodia, Ministry of Economy and Finance) employ financial/economic analysts
- **Private sector demand:** High — the largest source of Financial Analyst roles, concentrated in Phnom Penh
- **International organization demand:** Present — development banks, NGOs, and multinational companies operating in Cambodia hire financial analysts, often valuing English proficiency and international reporting standards experience
