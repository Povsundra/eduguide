# Career Entity: Business Analyst

## 1. Basic Information
- **Career name:** Business Analyst
- **Khmer name:** អ្នកវិភាគអាជីវកម្ម (Neak Vipheak Achivakam)
- **Career category:** Business / Information Systems
- **Industry:** Banking & Finance, Telecommunications, NGOs/Development, Higher Education, IT Services
- **Career level:** Entry to Senior (Intern/Junior Analyst → Business Analyst → Senior Business Analyst → Business Analysis Manager)
- **Alternative job titles:** Business Systems Analyst, IT Business Analyst, Process Analyst, Functional Analyst, Business Architect

## 2. Career Overview
A Business Analyst gathers and analyzes data to understand organizational challenges and recommend solutions that improve processes, systems, or business performance. The main purpose of the role is to bridge business needs and technical or operational solutions, translating stakeholder requirements into actionable recommendations. In Cambodia, this role appears across banking, telecom, NGOs/development projects, and higher-education institutions modernizing their operations. Within an organization, a Business Analyst typically works within an IT, operations, or strategy team, often reporting to a Department Head, Project Manager, or CIO, and liaising between business units and technical teams.

## 3. Responsibilities
- Gather, document, and analyze business requirements from stakeholders
- Analyze financial reports, market research, and operating procedures
- Identify inefficiencies and propose process or system improvements
- Liaise between business units (e.g., executives) and IT/technical teams
- Translate business needs into functional specifications for IT solutions
- Support the selection, implementation, and testing of new systems/technology
- Produce reports on findings, project progress, and implementation outcomes
- Prepare field surveys, collect and analyze data, and monitor business/financial performance (in project- or development-oriented roles)
- Assist quality assurance, documentation, and stakeholder sign-off processes

## 4. Required Skills

### Technical Skills
- Data analysis and data modeling
- Requirements gathering and documentation
- SQL / database querying
- Process mapping and business process improvement techniques
- Familiarity with IT systems, software implementation, and testing
- Report writing and presentation of findings
- Basic financial/market analysis (role-dependent)

### Soft Skills
- Communication and interpersonal skills across all organizational levels
- Stakeholder engagement and negotiation
- Problem-solving and critical thinking
- Attention to detail under tight deadlines
- Analytical mindset
- Networking and relationship management

## 5. Work Environment
- **Common workplaces:** Banks, telecommunications companies, NGOs/development organizations, universities/research institutions, IT service providers, government-linked projects
- **Office work:** Predominant
- **Remote work:** Occasional, depending on employer and project structure
- **Field work:** Present in development/NGO-oriented roles (e.g., site surveys, client data collection)
- **Laboratory work:** Not applicable

## 7. Job Outlook
- Business Analyst roles in Cambodia span diverse sectors — banking (e.g., IT Business Analyst positions at commercial banks), telecommunications, higher education/research institutions, and development-sector organizations working on water, electricity, and rural business projects.
- Demand for analysts who can bridge business and technology needs is rising as organizations pursue digital transformation, a pattern also seen regionally in Southeast Asia as companies integrate data analytics and, increasingly, AI into operations.
- Employers frequently value multilingual candidates (Khmer/English, and Khmer/English/Chinese in some sectors) given Cambodia's diverse business and investment landscape.
- No formal single qualification is mandatory, but a bachelor's degree in business, information systems, economics, or a related field is commonly expected, with certifications such as CBAP or PMI-PBA seen as credibility boosters internationally.

## 9. Cambodia Career Context
- **Common employers:** Commercial banks (e.g., ABA Bank), telecommunications companies (e.g., Cambodian SingMeng Telemedia), higher-education/research institutions (e.g., Institute of Technology of Cambodia project roles), NGOs and development organizations (e.g., water/sanitation and rural business support projects)
- **Industries hiring:** Banking and finance, telecommunications, higher education/research, international development/NGOs, IT services
- **Government demand:** Limited/indirect — more common through government-linked development projects (e.g., ARES/Belgium-supported ITC institutional project)
- **Private sector demand:** High — banks and telecom firms are prominent hirers, especially for IT Business Analyst roles
- **International organization demand:** Present — development-sector organizations (NGOs, donor-funded projects) hire business analysts for financial/business monitoring and reporting on client or beneficiary enterprises
