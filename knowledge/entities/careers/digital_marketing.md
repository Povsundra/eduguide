# Career Entity: Digital Marketing Specialist

## 1. Basic Information

- **Career name:** Digital Marketing Specialist
- **Khmer name:** អ្នកឯកទេសទីផ្សារឌីជីថល (Neak Ekkâtes Tiphsar Digital)
- **Career category:** Marketing & Communications
- **Industry:** Retail/E-commerce, Startups, Oil & Petroleum, Hospitality/Tourism, Professional Services, NGOs
- **Career level:** Entry to Senior
- **Alternative job titles:** Social Media Manager, SEO Analyst, Content Strategist, Digital Marketing Manager, Digital Marketing Executive, Performance Marketing Specialist

---

## 2. Career Overview

A Digital Marketing Specialist plans and executes online marketing activities that build brand awareness, engage audiences, and drive customer acquisition — spanning social media, search engine optimization (SEO), paid advertising, email marketing, and content strategy. The role uses digital channels and analytics to reach target audiences more precisely and measurably than traditional marketing methods.

- **Main purpose:** Grow an organization's online presence, audience engagement, and customer base through planned, data-informed digital marketing campaigns.
- **Importance:** <cite index="129-1">As Cambodia's digital economy expands rapidly, businesses are continuously looking for skilled digital marketers, with roles such as Digital Marketing Specialist, Social Media Manager, SEO Analyst, and Content Strategist in high demand</cite>, particularly as more commerce and brand engagement moves online.
- **Typical role within an organization:** Sits within a marketing or communications department, or works independently/freelance for smaller businesses; collaborates with content creators, designers, sales teams, and (in larger organizations) a Marketing Manager or Head of Marketing.

---

## 3. Responsibilities

- Plan and execute digital marketing campaigns across social media, search, and email channels
- Manage and grow organizational social media presence (content calendars, posting, community engagement)
- Run and optimize paid advertising campaigns (e.g., Facebook Ads, Google Ads)
- Perform search engine optimization (SEO) to improve website visibility and traffic
- Create or coordinate content (copy, graphics, video) for digital channels
- Manage email marketing campaigns, including newsletters and audience segmentation
- Analyze campaign performance using tools such as Google Analytics and report on key metrics (ROI, engagement, conversions)
- Maintain and update company websites or landing pages
- Stay current with digital marketing trends, platform changes, and best practices
- Coordinate with designers, content creators, and sales teams to align marketing efforts with business goals

**Typical decision-making responsibilities:** Choosing which channels and campaign types to prioritize for a given goal or budget, deciding how to adjust campaigns based on performance data, and determining messaging/targeting strategy for specific audience segments.

## 4. Required Skills

### Technical Skills
- Social media marketing and management (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, etc.)
- Search engine optimization (SEO) and search engine marketing (SEM)
- Paid advertising platforms (Facebook Ads, Google Ads)
- Email marketing tools and campaign management
- Web analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics)
- Basic content creation (copywriting, graphic design, or video editing familiarity)
- Website/CMS familiarity (e.g., WordPress) for landing pages and content updates
- Growing use of AI-powered marketing tools for content and campaign optimization

### Soft Skills
- Creativity
- Communication
- Analytical thinking (interpreting performance data)
- Time management (managing multiple campaigns/channels)
- Adaptability (fast-changing platforms and trends)
- Collaboration/teamwork with designers, content creators, and sales

## 5. Work Environment

**Typical workplaces:**
- Retail, e-commerce, and consumer brands
- Startups and small-to-medium businesses
- Oil, petroleum, and other large corporates with in-house marketing teams
- Hospitality and tourism companies
- Professional services and recruitment agencies
- NGOs (for donor communications and awareness campaigns)

**Work setting:**
- Primarily office-based, computer-intensive work
- Freelance and remote work are common in this field, including for international clients
- Field work and laboratory work are not part of this role, though occasional event or content-shoot attendance may occur
- Fast-paced, deadline- and trend-driven work environment

## 7. Job Outlook

- **Current demand:** High. <cite index="129-1">Digital Marketing Specialist and related roles are consistently identified as in high demand in Cambodia, offering salaries significantly higher than average entry-level positions for young, digitally native professionals</cite>.
- **Future demand:** <cite index="133-1">As businesses in Cambodia adapt to digital transformation, demand for professionals who can navigate and execute digital marketing strategies effectively continues to grow, with the country's startup ecosystem relying heavily on digital marketing to build brand presence and attract customers</cite>.
- **Emerging technologies:** AI-powered content generation and campaign optimization tools, increasing use of short-form video (TikTok, Reels) as a primary channel, growing sophistication in data analytics for marketing performance.
- **Industry trends:** Employers increasingly expect a combined skill set spanning social media, SEO, data analytics, and AI-powered tools rather than single-channel expertise; roles range from entry-level content/social execution to senior strategic positions overseeing SEO, SEM, and content marketing for large corporate clients.
- **Growth opportunities:** Startups and growing e-commerce brands offer fast-track opportunities to take on broad marketing ownership; larger corporates (including large industrial/energy companies with consumer-facing brands) hire dedicated digital marketing managers with more structured career paths; freelance and remote work for international clients is an accessible path for skilled specialists.

*(Salary figures found in local postings ranged widely by seniority and employer size — some junior/support roles listed a few hundred US dollars per month, while more senior digital marketing manager roles were unspecified/competitive — with no single authoritative Cambodia-wide salary source found; specific figures are therefore omitted per data-quality guidance.)*

## 9. Cambodia Career Context

- **Common employers in Cambodia:** Startups and e-commerce businesses, large corporates with consumer-facing brands (e.g., a Digital Marketing Manager role for a leading oil and petroleum company in Phnom Penh), recruitment and staffing agencies, and marketing/communications agencies.
- **Industries hiring this career:** Retail and e-commerce, hospitality and tourism, oil/petroleum and other large consumer-facing corporates, professional services and recruitment.
- **Government demand:** Limited direct evidence; role is concentrated in the private sector.
- **Private sector demand:** The dominant source of demand, spanning startups, established corporates, and marketing/recruitment agencies across multiple sectors.
- **International organization demand:** Present through NGOs needing digital communications and donor-engagement support, as well as through freelance/remote digital marketing work for international clients.
