- **Khmer name:** អ្នកវិទ្យាសាស្ត្រម្ហូបអាហារ (Neak Vityasastr Mhob Ahar)

- **Industry:** Food Manufacturing, Food Safety & Quality Control, Agriculture/Agri-processing, Government Regulatory Bodies
- **Career level:** Entry to Senior (Junior/QC Analyst → Food Scientist → Senior Food Scientist/QA Manager → R&D/Quality Director)
- **Alternative job titles:** Food Technologist, Quality Control/Quality Assurance Scientist, Food Safety Analyst, R&D Food Scientist

## 2. Career Overview
A Food Scientist studies the microbiological, physical, and chemical properties of food and ingredients to ensure safety, quality, and regulatory compliance, while also supporting new product development. The main purpose of the role is to protect consumer health and improve food quality, shelf life, and processing efficiency. In Cambodia, this role is important given the country's growing food manufacturing and agri-processing sectors and its efforts to strengthen food safety standards for both domestic consumption and export. Within an organization, a Food Scientist typically works in a Quality Control/Quality Assurance or R&D department, reporting to a QA/QC Manager, Plant Manager, or Head of R&D, and collaborating with microbiologists, engineers, and packaging specialists.

## 3. Responsibilities
- Evaluate the nutritional value, color, flavor, and texture of food products
- Test food samples for harmful molds, yeast, bacteria, and contaminants
- Ensure manufacturing processes comply with government, industry, and consumer standards
- Support the development of new food products, from concept to commercialization
- Establish and monitor food safety and quality control procedures (e.g., HACCP, GMP, GHP)
- Work with production, packaging, and supply chain teams to maintain product standards
- Conduct sensory testing and shelf-life/stability studies
- Prepare technical documentation, ingredient statements, and compliance reports
- Investigate and help set standards for food safety at the plant or company level

## 7. Job Outlook
- Cambodia's food manufacturing and agri-processing sectors are still relatively small compared to regional neighbors but are gradually expanding, with government reporting greater export volumes and growing opportunities for processing and packaging plants that add value to raw agricultural products.
- Cambodia is developing its first dedicated Food Safety Law (in progress with FAO support), alongside existing frameworks such as Prakas 868 and the Law on Management of Quality and Safety of Products and Services (2018), reflecting increasing regulatory attention to food safety.
- CAMCONTROL (under the Ministry of Commerce) operates a network of laboratories inspecting food imports/exports, creating institutional demand for food safety/quality science expertise.
- Rising urban consumer demand for processed, meat, and dairy products, alongside export diversification goals (e.g., cashew processing policy, agricultural exports reaching $5 billion), point to growing need for food scientists in both quality control and product development.
- Food safety technology and expertise remain in high demand as Cambodia works to meet Sanitary and Phytosanitary (SPS) standards for export diversification.

## 9. Cambodia Career Context
- **Common employers:** Food manufacturing and agri-processing companies (e.g., large agribusiness/food companies operating in Cambodia), government regulatory bodies (CAMCONTROL under the Ministry of Commerce, Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries), export-oriented food processors
- **Industries hiring:** Food manufacturing, agriculture/agri-processing, food import-export inspection, government regulatory agencies
- **Government demand:** Present — CAMCONTROL and MAFF employ inspectors and technical staff for food safety and SPS compliance
- **Private sector demand:** Growing — agribusiness and food processing companies (e.g., in the poultry, seasoning/sauce, and agri-export sectors) hire food scientists/technologists for QC and product development
- **International organization demand:** Present — FAO and other development partners support Cambodia's food safety law development and capacity building, sometimes involving technical food safety experts
