# Career Entity: Full Stack Developer

## 1. Basic Information

- **Career name:** Full Stack Developer
- **Khmer name:** អ្នកអភិវឌ្ឍន៍កម្មវិធីពេញលេញ (Neak Aphivath Kamviti Penh Leñ)
- **Career category:** Technology / Software Engineering
- **Industry:** Information Technology, E-commerce, Banking & Finance, Startups, Digital Agencies
- **Career level:** Entry to Senior
- **Alternative job titles:** Full Stack Software Engineer, Full Stack Web Developer, Web Application Developer

---

## 2. Career Overview

A Full Stack Developer builds both the client-facing (front-end) and server-side (back-end) parts of web or software applications, along with the databases and infrastructure that connect them. Unlike specialists who focus only on front-end or only on back-end work, a Full Stack Developer can take an application from user interface design through to server logic, database integration, and deployment.

- **Main purpose:** Independently build and maintain complete, end-to-end web/software applications, reducing the need for separate front-end and back-end specialists on smaller teams.
- **Importance:** <cite index="35-1">Full-stack developers are in especially high demand in Cambodia for their ability to handle both front-end and back-end development, making them valuable for companies building comprehensive digital solutions</cite>.
- **Typical role within an organization:** Works within a product or engineering team, often at startups, digital agencies, or IT service companies where versatility across the stack is valued; may own a feature or product end-to-end rather than a single layer of it.

---

## 3. Responsibilities

- Design and build user interfaces using front-end technologies (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, frameworks such as React, Angular, or Vue.js)
- Develop server-side logic and APIs using back-end languages/frameworks (e.g., Node.js, Django, Laravel, Spring)
- Design, integrate, and manage databases (SQL and/or NoSQL)
- Build and maintain RESTful APIs connecting front-end and back-end systems
- Implement authentication, security, and access-control features
- Manage deployment pipelines and basic server/infrastructure configuration
- Test and debug applications across the full stack
- Collaborate with project managers, designers, and other developers on end-to-end delivery
- Write technical and user-facing documentation
- Maintain and improve existing applications (bug fixes, performance, refactoring)

**Typical decision-making responsibilities:** Choosing the front-end/back-end technology stack for a project, structuring the database schema, deciding how front-end and back-end components should communicate, and balancing speed of delivery against code quality across the whole application.

---

## 4. Required Skills

### Technical Skills
- Front-end development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript/TypeScript, React/Angular/Vue.js)
- Back-end development (Node.js, Python/Django, PHP/Laravel, Java/Spring, C#, or Ruby on Rails)
- Database management (SQL databases such as MySQL/PostgreSQL; NoSQL databases such as MongoDB/Firebase)
- RESTful API design and integration
- Version control (Git)
- Basic DevOps/deployment knowledge (server management, deployment pipelines)
- Authentication and application security basics
- Testing and debugging across the stack

### Soft Skills
- Problem solving
- Adaptability (switching between front-end and back-end contexts)
- Communication (with project managers, designers, and clients)
- Time management (balancing multiple layers of a project)
- Attention to detail
- Independence/self-direction (able to own a feature end-to-end)

## 5. Work Environment

**Typical workplaces:**
- IT service and software development companies
- Startups and digital agencies
- E-commerce companies
- Banks and fintech companies
- Freelance/contract work, including remote work for international clients

**Work setting:**
- Primarily office-based, computer-intensive work
- Freelance and remote work are notably common in this career in Cambodia, both for local IT service companies and international clients
- Field work and laboratory work are not part of this role

## 7. Job Outlook

- **Current demand:** High. <cite index="41-1">Full stack developer positions are increasingly in demand as Cambodian businesses of all sizes rapidly digitize, with employers valuing the versatility full stack engineers bring across many parts of a project</cite>.
- **Future demand:** <cite index="35-1">Cambodia's digital economy is projected to contribute meaningfully to national GDP by 2030, and a projected shortage of IT professionals is expected to keep skilled developers, including full stack developers, in a strong negotiating position</cite>.
- **Emerging technologies:** AI-assisted coding tools, integration of AI/LLM features into web applications, progressive web apps, cloud-native deployment practices.
- **Industry trends:** <cite index="40-1">Companies in technology, software development, and e-commerce in Phnom Penh are actively hiring full stack developers with expertise in stacks such as React, Node.js, and PostgreSQL</cite>.
- **Growth opportunities:** Strong local demand from startups and IT service companies; freelance and remote opportunities with international clients are a well-established path for full stack developers based in Cambodia; government-backed digital initiatives (e.g., startup and digital-economy support programs) add further momentum.

## 9. Cambodia Career Context

- **Common employers in Cambodia:** IT service companies (e.g., E-Solutions Cambodia), digital product and software firms, e-commerce and fintech startups, and international clients hiring Cambodia-based freelance or remote full stack developers.
- **Industries hiring this career:** Technology, software development, e-commerce, banking/fintech, and digital agencies.
- **Government demand:** Indirect — supported by government initiatives promoting the digital economy and startup ecosystem (e.g., startup support centers) rather than direct government hiring.
- **Private sector demand:** The dominant source of demand, particularly from startups and IT service companies in Phnom Penh building web and mobile products.
- **International organization demand:** Present mainly through freelance and remote work arrangements with international companies and clients rather than through international-organization offices in Cambodia.
