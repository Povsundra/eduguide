curriculum_overview:

  educational_structure:
    - Common Engineering Foundation Program (Years 1–2)
    - Third Year (Core Civil Engineering)
    - Fourth Year (Advanced Civil Engineering)
    - Fifth Year (Professional Civil Engineering)
    - Laboratory-based Learning
    - Field Practice
    - Industrial Internship
    - Final Engineering Project

  learning_progression:

    foundation: >
      During the first two years, students complete the Common Engineering
      Foundation Program shared by all engineering departments at the
      Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC). The curriculum develops
      strong foundations in mathematics, natural sciences, engineering
      fundamentals, technical communication, and languages before students
      enter the Civil Engineering specialization.

    specialization: >
      Beginning in Year 3, students study the fundamental disciplines of
      civil engineering including engineering mechanics, construction
      materials, surveying, structural analysis, soil mechanics,
      hydraulics, transportation engineering, and construction technology.

    concentration: >
      Students advance into reinforced concrete design, steel structures,
      foundation engineering, bridge engineering, highway engineering,
      hydraulic engineering, water resources, construction management,
      quantity surveying, infrastructure design, and project management.

    experiential_learning: >
      Students complete lectures (Cours), tutorials (Travaux Dirigés),
      laboratory practical sessions (Travaux Pratiques), engineering
      design projects, surveying camps, construction site visits,
      industrial internship, and a final engineering project.
