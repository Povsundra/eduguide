notes:
  - This is a Dual Degree curriculum jointly delivered by AUPP and the University of Arizona (UA).
  - UA Major courses focus primarily on Communication and Public Relations.
  - AUPP Major courses integrate business, management, finance, marketing, and communication.
  - Students complete approved upper-division electives to satisfy the 12-credit elective requirement.
  - Internship (INTS 400) is compulsory and requires completion of at least 84 credits before enrollment.
  - The internship includes a minimum of 160 hours of supervised workplace experience.
  - AUPP follows a flexible credit-based curriculum rather than a fixed semester-by-semester curriculum.
