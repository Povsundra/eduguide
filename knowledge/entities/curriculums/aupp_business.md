curriculum_overview:
  educational_structure:
    - General Education
    - Business Foundation
    - Business Major
    - Business Major Electives
    - Free Electives

  learning_progression:
    foundation:
      Students complete General Education together with Business Foundation courses including accounting, economics, statistics, business communication, and information management.

    specialization:
      Students complete Business Major courses covering finance, management, marketing, entrepreneurship, leadership, operations, business law, ethics, and strategic management.

    concentration:
      Students complete Business Major Electives in specialized business areas such as marketing analytics, digital marketing, supply chain management, and data visualization.

    experiential_learning:
      Students complete a compulsory Internship and are encouraged to apply classroom knowledge through practical business projects and professional experiences.

notes:
  - AUPP follows a flexible credit-based curriculum rather than a fixed semester-by-semester curriculum.
  - Business Foundation courses must be completed before enrolling in Business Major courses or Business Major electives.
  - Students register for courses each semester with guidance from an academic advisor.
  - Business Major courses are compulsory.
  - Students select approved Business Major electives to satisfy the 15-credit specialization requirement.
  - Students complete 18 credits of upper-division Free Electives.
  - Internship (INTS 400) is a compulsory graduation requirement.
  - Information Management Systems Lab (BUSN 370L) is the only required laboratory component.
