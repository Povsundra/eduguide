curriculum_overview:
  educational_structure:
    - Foundation Year
    - Second Year
    - Third Year
    - Fourth Year
    - Internship
    - Final Project or Research Thesis

  learning_progression:
    foundation:
      Students build strong foundations in mathematics, chemistry, biology, physics, computer programming, statistics, engineering fundamentals, and academic skills.

    specialization:
      Students study analytical chemistry, biochemistry, microbiology, food engineering, biotechnology, bio-process engineering, quality assurance, and computational engineering.

    concentration:
      Students advance into molecular biology, gene technology, plant tissue culture, immunology, environmental engineering, toxicology, and biomedical engineering.

    experiential_learning:
      Students complete laboratory-based courses, project practicum every year, internship, and either a Final Project or a Research Thesis/Engineering Training.
