curriculum_overview:

  educational_structure:
    - Common Engineering Foundation Program (Years 1–2)
    - Third Year (Core Data Science)
    - Fourth Year (Advanced Data Science)
    - Fifth Year (Applied Data Science)
    - Laboratory-based Learning
    - Industrial Internship

  learning_progression:

    foundation:
      During the first two years, students complete the Common Engineering Foundation Program shared by all engineering departments at the Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC). The curriculum develops strong foundations in mathematics, natural sciences, engineering fundamentals, languages, and professional skills before students enter their departmental specialization.

    specialization:
      Beginning in Year 3, students study the foundations of data science including programming, mathematical modeling, statistics, optimization, databases, machine learning, and computer networks.

    concentration:
      Students advance into artificial intelligence, statistical learning, graph theory, distributed computing, cloud computing, data visualization, natural language processing, information retrieval, time series forecasting, and big data analytics.

    experiential_learning:
      Students complete lectures (Cours), tutorials (Travaux Dirigés), practical laboratory sessions (Travaux Pratiques), programming assignments, major projects, and a compulsory Final Year Industrial Internship.

notes:
  - The Data Science curriculum follows ITC's five-year engineering education model.
  - The first two years are the Common Engineering Foundation Program shared by all engineering departments.
  - The departmental curriculum begins in Year 3 (Semester V).
  - Most technical courses include lectures (Cours), tutorials (Travaux Dirigés), and practical laboratory sessions (Travaux Pratiques).
  - Students complete both a Minor Project and a Major Project before undertaking the compulsory Final Year Internship.
  - The official curriculum does not specify the overall program credit requirement.
