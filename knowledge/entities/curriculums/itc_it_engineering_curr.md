curriculum_overview:

  educational_structure:
    - Common Engineering Foundation Program (Years 1–2)
    - Third Year (Department Specialization)
    - Fourth Year (Advanced Specialization)
    - Fifth Year (Advanced Topics and Industrial Internship)
    - Laboratory-based Learning
    - Industrial Internship

  learning_progression:

    foundation:
      During the first two years, students complete the Common Engineering Foundation Program shared by all engineering departments at the Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC). The curriculum develops strong foundations in mathematics, natural sciences, engineering fundamentals, languages, and professional skills before students enter their departmental specialization.

    specialization:
      Beginning in Semester V (Year 3), students study Information and Communication Engineering, including programming, algorithms, databases, computer systems, networking, software engineering, telecommunications, and information systems.

    concentration:
      Students advance into computer architecture, operating systems, web technologies, network administration, distributed systems, cloud computing, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, data mining, natural language processing, and mobile application development.

    experiential_learning:
      Students complete lecture-based (Cours), tutorial (Travaux Dirigés), and laboratory (Travaux Pratiques) courses throughout Years 3–5, culminating in a compulsory Final Year Industrial Internship.
