# scholarship_aupp_academic_excellence_2026

## Basic Information

- Scholarship Name: AUPP Academic Excellence Scholarship
- Type: Merit Scholarship
- Provider: American University of Phnom Penh
- University: American University of Phnom Penh
- Academic Year: 2026
- Status: Ongoing
- Official Page:
  https://www.aupp.edu.kh/aupp-scholarships/aupp-academic-excellence-scholarships/

### Overview

Merit-based scholarship awarded to students achieving Grade A or Grade B on the Cambodian National High School Examination.

### Coverage

Single Degree

- Grade A
  - 35% (Top 7)
  - 30% (Top 6)
  - 25% (Top 5)
  - 20% (Top 4)
  - 15% (Top 3)
  - 10% (Top 2)
  - 5% (Top 1)

Duration

- 4 Years

Dual Degree

- Same percentage
- Duration: 2 Years

### Benefits

- Tuition discount
- Merit recognition

### Eligibility

- Grade A or Grade B National Exam
- Admission to AUPP
- All majors eligible

### English Requirement

One of

- IELTS ≥ 6.0
- TOEFL iBT ≥ 79
- Duolingo ≥ 110

Otherwise

- English Placement Test (EPT)

### Required Documents

- National ID or Passport
- National Exam Result
- English Proficiency Certificate (if available)

### Application Information

- Complete AUPP Admission Application
- Pay US$35 non-refundable application fee
- Submit scholarship application

### Eligible Programs

- All undergraduate majors

### Official Source

https://www.aupp.edu.kh/aupp-scholarships/aupp-academic-excellence-scholarships/

# scholarship_techo_digital_talent_2026

## Basic Information

- Type: Government Scholarship
- Provider: Ministry of Post and Telecommunications (MPTC)
- Partner University: AUPP
- Status: Closed (latest published cycle)

### Coverage

- Full Scholarship
- Tuition
- Administration Fee
- Application Fee
- English Preparatory Program (eligible students)

### Eligibility

- Grade A, B or C National Exam
- New applicants only
- Current AUPP students are not eligible

### Eligible Programs

Single Degree

- Artificial Intelligence
- Cybersecurity
- Digital Infrastructure
- Information & Communications Technology
- Software Development

Dual Degree

- Information Technology Management / Computer Science
- Information Systems
- Web & Mobile Application Development

### Additional Note

Dual Degree students pay an additional US$3,000/year because the scholarship only covers the single-degree tuition component.

### Required Documents

- National ID
- National Exam Result
- English Certificate
- House Photos
- Google Maps Location

### Official Source

https://www.aupp.edu.kh/aupp-scholarships/techo-digital-talent-scholarship/
