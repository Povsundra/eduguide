### Overview

The Techo Digital Talent Scholarship is a government-funded scholarship established by the Ministry of Post and Telecommunications (MPTC) to develop highly skilled digital professionals for Cambodia's digital economy.

For the 2026–2027 academic year, the program provides **600 undergraduate scholarships** across seven partner universities, including CADT. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

### Coverage

- Full Tuition Scholarship
- Undergraduate Study
- Digital Technology Programs

Other financial benefits:

- Not specified

Duration:

- Based on the selected undergraduate program

---

### Eligibility

Official page specifies:

- Admission to one of the participating universities
- Meet university admission requirements
- Complete scholarship application

Additional eligibility requirements are published during each admission cycle.

---

### Eligible Programs

Digital technology-related undergraduate programs offered by CADT.

Examples include:

- Computer Science
- Artificial Intelligence
- Data Science
- Cybersecurity
- Software Engineering
- Digital Business
- Other digital technology majors

(Program availability depends on the academic year.)
