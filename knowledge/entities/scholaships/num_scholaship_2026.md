### Overview

The Maybank Cambodia Scholarship Programme supports outstanding Cambodian undergraduate students studying at selected Cambodian universities, including the National University of Management (NUM). It aims to develop future leaders in business, finance, banking, and digital technology. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

### Coverage

The scholarship provides:

- Full Tuition Fees
- Enrollment Fees
- Laptop
- Book Allowance
- Living Allowance
- Insurance Coverage
- Internship Opportunity
- Employment Opportunity after Graduation (Bonded)

---

### Eligibility

Applicants must:

- Be Cambodian citizens
- Study at an eligible university
- Be in Year 1 or Year 2 (Semester 1)
- Be under 22 years old
- Maintain a minimum GPA of 3.0
- Have household income not exceeding USD 24,000 per year

---

### Eligible Programs

Business-related programs

- Business Administration
- Accounting
- Finance
- Banking
- Economics
- Law
- Psychology

Digital-related programs

- Information Technology
- Programming
- Data Science
- Artificial Intelligence
- Information Security
- Creative Design

# Notes

Unlike AUPP, ITC, and CADT, NUM currently does **not** publish a dedicated scholarship page or university-administered scholarship catalogue.

Most scholarships available to NUM students are provided by:

- Government ministries
- Banks
- Companies
- NGOs
- International organizations

Students should monitor:

- NUM official announcements
- Scholarship provider announcements
- Ministry announcements

for updated scholarship opportunities.
