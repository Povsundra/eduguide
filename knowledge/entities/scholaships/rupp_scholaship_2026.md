### Overview

RUPP participates in Erasmus+ projects with European partner universities. Selected students may receive funding for exchange studies, internships, or staff mobility.

---

### Coverage

- Tuition waiver
- Travel support
- Monthly allowance
- Insurance

### Eligibility

- Good academic standing
- English proficiency

# Notes

Unlike ITC, CADT, and AUPP, RUPP does not currently operate a centralized university scholarship system.

Most scholarships available through RUPP are:

- Government scholarships
- International exchange scholarships
- Development partner scholarships
- Faculty-specific scholarship projects

Students should monitor:

- RUPP International Relations Office
- Individual faculty announcements
- MoEYS
- Development partner websites

for new scholarship opportunities.
