# Program Entity

## 1. Basic Information

- Program name: Bachelor of Computer Science and Engineering
- Degree: Bachelor's Degree
- Faculty: Faculty of Science
- Department: Department of Computer Science
- University: Royal University of Phnom Penh (RUPP)
- Duration: 4 years (8 semesters)
- Language: English (technical and research communication emphasized throughout the program)
- Study mode: Full-time

---

## 2. Program Overview

### Purpose
The Bachelor of Computer Science and Engineering program prepares students with strong theoretical foundations and practical skills in computer science, software development, networking, and modern information technologies to support Cambodia's digital transformation and the Fourth Industrial Revolution (Industry 4.0).

### Focus
- Computer Science
- Software Engineering
- Information Systems
- Computer Networks
- Artificial Intelligence
- Data Science
- Database Systems
- Web and Mobile Application Development
- Smart Technologies

### Educational Goals
The program aims to produce graduates who can:
- Design and develop software systems.
- Design, administer, and secure computer networks.
- Analyze and develop information systems.
- Apply AI and emerging technologies to solve real-world problems.
- Demonstrate ethical professionalism, innovation, and lifelong learning.
- Contribute to Cambodia's digital economy and technological development.
