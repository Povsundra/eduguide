# Program Entity

## 1. Basic Information

- Program name: Bachelor of Science in Mathematics
- Degree: Bachelor of Science (BSc)
- Faculty: Faculty of Science
- Department: Department of Mathematics
- University: Royal University of Phnom Penh (RUPP)
- Duration: 4 years
- Language: English and Khmer (English language courses are integrated throughout the curriculum)
- Study mode: Full-time (In-house undergraduate program)

---

## 2. Program Overview

### Purpose

The Bachelor of Science in Mathematics provides students with a strong foundation in pure mathematics, applied mathematics, mathematical modelling, and programming. The program prepares graduates for careers in education, science, engineering, finance, research, and other data-driven industries.

### Focus

- Pure Mathematics
- Applied Mathematics
- Mathematical Modelling
- Programming
- Statistics
- Computational Mathematics
- Mathematical Applications
- Machine Learning Foundations
- Artificial Intelligence Foundations

### Educational Goals

The program aims to:

- Develop leaders in mathematics and applied mathematics.
- Educate professional mathematicians, including secondary school teachers.
- Prepare graduates for research institutes in science and engineering.
- Strengthen the application of mathematics across multidisciplinary fields.
- Introduce mathematical foundations for machine learning and artificial intelligence.

## 7. Learning Outcomes

Graduates will be able to:

- Model natural and social phenomena using mathematics and statistics.
- Apply programming languages (e.g., Python and MATLAB) to solve mathematical and applied problems.
- Analyze and solve mathematical problems using rigorous methods.
- Apply mathematical and statistical techniques in engineering, economics, science, and industry.
- Conduct mathematical research and present research findings.
- Prepare technical reports with appropriate academic references.
- Use computational tools for modelling and numerical analysis.
- Select and communicate appropriate mathematical methods for prediction and decision-making.
- Apply ICT tools ethically and responsibly.
- Work independently and collaboratively while demonstrating lifelong learning.
