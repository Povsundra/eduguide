## 2. Program Overview

### Purpose

The Bachelor of Bio-Engineering program equips students with interdisciplinary knowledge in biology, engineering, and technology to develop innovative solutions for healthcare, agriculture, environmental sustainability, food production, and biotechnology. The program prepares graduates to meet Cambodia's growing demand for bio-engineering professionals and researchers.

### Focus

- Bio-Engineering
- Biotechnology
- Biomedical Engineering
- Biological Systems
- Environmental Engineering
- Agricultural Technology
- Food Technology
- Bioprocess Engineering
- Biomaterials
- Engineering Design

### Educational Goals

The program aims to:

- Develop engineers with strong biological and engineering foundations.
- Integrate engineering principles with life sciences.
- Prepare graduates for research, innovation, and industrial applications.
- Promote sustainable technological development.
- Produce ethical professionals capable of solving real-world biological and engineering challenges.
