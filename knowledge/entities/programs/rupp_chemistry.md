# Program Entity

## 1. Basic Information

- Program name: Bachelor of Science in Chemistry
- Degree: Bachelor of Science (BSc)
- Faculty: Faculty of Science
- Department: Department of Chemistry
- University: Royal University of Phnom Penh (RUPP)
- Duration: 4 years
- Language: Khmer and English (English is used for scientific literature and technical communication)
- Study mode: Full-time (In-house undergraduate program)

---

## 2. Program Overview

### Purpose

The Bachelor of Science in Chemistry program provides students with comprehensive knowledge of chemistry through theoretical instruction and laboratory practice. The program prepares graduates to contribute to education, scientific research, industry, environmental management, healthcare, agriculture, and national development.

### Focus

- General Chemistry
- Organic Chemistry
- Inorganic Chemistry
- Analytical Chemistry
- Physical Chemistry
- Biochemistry
- Environmental Chemistry
- Industrial Chemistry
- Chemical Analysis
- Laboratory Science

### Educational Goals

The program aims to:

- Develop strong scientific knowledge in chemistry.
- Build practical laboratory and analytical skills.
- Prepare graduates for careers in research, education, industry, healthcare, and environmental science.
- Promote scientific research and innovation.
- Develop ethical, responsible, and lifelong learners who contribute to Cambodia's scientific and technological development.

- Conduct scientific research.

## 7. Learning Outcomes

Graduates will be able to:
