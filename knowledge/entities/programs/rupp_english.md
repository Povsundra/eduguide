## 2. Program Overview

### Purpose

The Bachelor of Education in Teaching English as a Foreign Language (BEd TEFL) prepares students to become qualified English language teachers, educators, researchers, and communication professionals. The program develops advanced English proficiency together with pedagogical knowledge, educational research skills, and professional ethics.

### Focus

- English Language Proficiency
- English Language Teaching (ELT)
- Teaching English as a Foreign Language (TEFL)
- Linguistics
- Applied Linguistics
- Literature
- Curriculum and Instruction
- Educational Psychology
- Language Assessment
- Educational Research

### Educational Goals

The program aims to:

- Develop highly competent English language teachers.
- Build strong communication skills in listening, speaking, reading, and writing.
- Prepare graduates with modern teaching methodologies.
- Promote educational research and lifelong learning.
- Produce ethical professionals who contribute to improving English education in Cambodia.

- Conduct classroom-based educational research.
