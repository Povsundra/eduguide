# 2. University Overview

## History

The Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC) was established in 1964. Originally founded as the Institut Technique Supérieur de l’Amitié Khméro–Soviétique (ITSAKS), the institution was developed with support from the former Soviet Union. After Cambodia's reconstruction, ITC was renovated through cooperation between the Cambodian and French governments beginning in 1993. Since 2004, ITC has operated under Cambodian management through the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport. :contentReference[oaicite:12]{index=12}

## Vision

ITC aspires to become the leading university in Cambodia in science, technology, engineering, education, and research, while achieving regional excellence in these fields. :contentReference[oaicite:13]{index=13}

## Mission

The university's missions are to:

- Produce talented and highly skilled graduates in science, technology, and engineering.
- Advance applied research that promotes innovation, entrepreneurship, and technology transfer to support Cambodia's development. :contentReference[oaicite:14]{index=14}

## Educational Focus

ITC specializes in engineering, science, technology, architecture, and applied research. It emphasizes strong scientific foundations, practical engineering skills, innovation, and industry engagement. :contentReference[oaicite:15]{index=15}

## Reputation

ITC is widely recognized as Cambodia's leading public higher education institution specializing in engineering and technology. It has played a major role in developing the country's engineering workforce for more than six decades. :contentReference[oaicite:16]{index=16}

## Role in Cambodia

ITC contributes to Cambodia's socio-economic development by:

- Developing highly qualified engineers and technical professionals.
- Conducting applied research.
- Supporting technology transfer.
- Collaborating with government, industry, and international institutions. :contentReference[oaicite:17]{index=17}
