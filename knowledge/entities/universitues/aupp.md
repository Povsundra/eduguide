# 2. University Overview

## History

The American University of Phnom Penh (AUPP) was established in 2013 as Cambodia's first university to provide an American-style higher education system. The university was founded to offer internationally recognized education that prepares graduates with the knowledge, skills, and leadership qualities needed to succeed in a global environment. Since its establishment, AUPP has expanded its academic offerings, strengthened international partnerships, and invested in modern campus facilities.

**Source**

- https://www.aupp.edu.kh/about-aupp/

To become Cambodia's leading university recognized internationally for academic excellence, innovation, leadership, and global engagement.
