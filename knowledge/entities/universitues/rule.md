# 2. University Overview

## History

The Royal University of Law and Economics (RULE) is the oldest higher education institution in Cambodia, originally founded in 1949 as the National Institute of Law, Politics and Economic Sciences. After the civil war, it was re-established in 1980 as the Faculty of Law and Economics. In 2003, it was officially elevated to university status and renamed the Royal University of Law and Economics. It has historically been heavily supported by French academic cooperation.

## Vision

To be the leading and most prestigious university in Cambodia in the fields of Law, Economics, Public Administration, and Business Management, recognized regionally for its academic excellence.

## Mission

To provide high-quality education and training to develop competent, ethical, and socially responsible human resources. RULE aims to equip students with rigorous academic knowledge and professional skills to serve the legal, economic, and administrative sectors of Cambodia.

## Educational Focus

RULE focuses strictly on the social sciences, specifically Law, Public Administration, International Relations, Economics, Finance, and Business Management. It features specialized international tracks (English and French based) alongside its national curriculum.

## Reputation

RULE is universally recognized as Cambodia’s premier institution for legal education and public administration. It is the top choice for students aspiring to become lawyers, judges, diplomats, and high-level civil servants.

## Role in Cambodia

The university serves as the primary pipeline for Cambodia's judicial system, government ministries, and corporate legal/financial sectors. It heavily influences national legal frameworks and public policy through its faculty expertise and alumni network.

- Public Administration and Public Policy

- Law (National and International)
- Public Administration

- Economics
- Business and Financial Management

## Teaching Approach

RULE combines traditional theoretical lectures with practical application. It strongly emphasizes case studies, legal research, moot court simulations, and clinical legal education to prepare students for professional environments. 

## Research Orientation

Research at RULE is heavily oriented toward legal frameworks, human rights, public policy, and socio-economic development. The university encourages legal publications, comparative law studies, and public administration research.

# 6. Student Experience

## Student Clubs

RULE hosts numerous active student organizations, including the RULE Debate Club, English Club, French Club, and various youth union federations.

## Competitions

RULE students are highly active in international academic competitions, most notably the Philip C. Jessup International Law Moot Court Competition, the Willem C. Vis International Commercial Arbitration Moot, and client counseling competitions.

## Exchange Opportunities

The university boasts one of Cambodia’s highest rates of outbound student mobility, particularly to France, Japan, and European nations through Erasmus+ and bilateral agreements.

## Career Center

The university integrates career orientation through its Employability Centers and extensive alumni network, hosting job fairs specifically targeted at law firms, banks, and NGOs.

## International Office

RULE operates distinct international program departments (e.g., French Cooperation Department, English Language Based Bachelor of Law office) that manage foreign faculty, dual degrees, and international student affairs.

## Student Support Services

- Legal writing and research support
- Academic advising for international dual-degree tracks
- Foreign language support (French and English legal terminology)

# 7. International Collaboration

## International Organizations

- Agence Universitaire de la Francophonie (AUF)
- Raoul Wallenberg Institute of Human Rights and Humanitarian Law (RWI)
- Erasmus+ (European Union)

## Partner Universities

RULE has an exceptionally strong network of international partners, especially in France (e.g., Université Lumière Lyon 2, Université Paris 2 Panthéon-Assas, Audencia Business School), as well as universities in Japan, Korea, and the ASEAN region.

## Exchange Programs

Facilitated extensively through French Cooperation agreements and the Erasmus+ mobility framework for both students and faculty.

## Dual Degree Programs

RULE is renowned for its Dual Degree programs. Students in specific tracks can graduate with a Cambodian degree from RULE and a recognized European degree from partner institutions in France or Belgium (available at both Bachelor's and Master's levels).

## Industry Partnerships

The university holds memorandums of understanding (MoUs) with leading national and international law firms, commercial banks, and auditing firms to facilitate internships and curriculum alignment.
