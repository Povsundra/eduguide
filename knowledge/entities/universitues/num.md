# 2. University Overview

## History

The National University of Management (NUM) traces its origins to 1983 when it was established as the Economics Science Institute. It evolved over the years, launching its first graduate program in Business Management in 1999 in cooperation with Universiti Utara Malaysia (UUM). In 2003, the School of Graduate Studies was officially established by the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport, and the institution transitioned into a comprehensive national university.

## Vision

“Leading Research University” — The university’s vision emphasizes improving and applying the best quality standards in education, research, and innovation, with a strong commitment to becoming the leading research university in entrepreneurship and innovation in Cambodia.

## Mission

“Cultivate Innovative Leaders, Managers, and Entrepreneurs for Society.” NUM is firmly committed to the development of competent and socially responsible human resources equipped with high intellectual knowledge, skills, and ethics to contribute productively to Cambodia's socio-economic development.

## Educational Focus

NUM focuses heavily on business, management, economics, law, public policy, and digital technologies (including information technology, robotics, and artificial intelligence). It aligns its programs with the demands of the digital economy and entrepreneurship.

## Reputation

NUM is widely recognized as one of Cambodia's top public business and management schools. It has built a strong reputation for fostering a culture of innovation, startup incubation, and research excellence among its students and faculty.

## Role in Cambodia

The university plays a critical role in Cambodia's socio-economic advancement by cultivating business leaders, policymakers, and tech innovators. It actively bridges the gap between academia, the business community, and government policymakers through policy research and technology transfer.

## Teaching Approach

NUM adopts a dynamic and cutting-edge teaching approach that combines theoretical foundations with practical, work-based learning. It highly encourages peer-to-peer learning, mentorship, hands-on projects, workshops, and integrating technology with cultural education to foster creativity and critical problem-solving.

## Research Orientation

The university actively promotes policy research, interdisciplinary studies, and innovation. Through its strategic plan, teaching, learning, and research are treated as interconnected pillars, with strong encouragement for staff and students to engage in projects that address real-world socio-economic and technological issues.

# 5. Research & Innovation

## Research Centers

- ASEAN Economic Research Institution

## Innovation Labs

- Digital Technology Center (DTC)

## Major Research Fields

- Economic Policy and Development
- Social Innovation and Entrepreneurship
- Digital Technology (AI, Machine Learning, Robotics)
- Public Policy

## Industry Collaboration

NUM strongly emphasizes collaboration with the private sector. Through centers like CERI and DTC, it bridges academia and the business community. It partners with domestic telecoms (e.g., SMART Axiata) and enterprise innovators to support tech product development and commercialization.

## International Research

The university engages in global research through the Office of External Research Projects, actively executing policy research projects with UN agencies (World Bank, ADB, UNDP) and managing Erasmus Plus research initiatives.
