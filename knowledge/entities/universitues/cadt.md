# 2. University Overview

## History

The Cambodia Academy of Digital Technology (CADT) was established in 2014. It originally operated as the National Institute of Posts, Telecoms & ICT (NIPTICT). The institution launched its professional training center in 2015 and graduated its first cohort of students in 2018. It was later rebranded as CADT to reflect its expanded mandate as a national academy driving Cambodia's digital transformation.

## Vision

To be the flagship public education and research institution, developing excellent digital talent and innovators, driving Cambodia toward a digital society.

## Mission

To provide education, training, research, and innovation in digital technology to support the development of Cambodia’s digital government, economy, and society.

## Educational Focus

CADT is strictly focused on digital technology, digital governance, telecommunications, and applied technological innovation. It acts as the primary training hub for both tech students and government civil servants.

## Reputation

CADT is recognized as the national flagship research and education institution for digital technology in Cambodia, operating directly under the Ministry of Post and Telecommunications (MPTC). 

## Role in Cambodia

CADT plays a critical role in Cambodia’s digital ecosystem by developing the human capital required to implement the country's national digital economy and digital government policies. It bridges the gap between academia, government administration, and the private tech sector.

## Teaching Approach

CADT heavily emphasizes work-based learning, practical skills, and direct industry alignment. The curriculum is designed to produce highly competent, work-ready graduates for the tech sector through hands-on labs and project-based learning.

## Research Orientation

The institution is highly focused on applied Research and Development (R&D) aimed at solving real-world local problems. Key research areas include healthcare AI, Internet of Things (IoT), and Khmer Natural Language Processing (NLP).
