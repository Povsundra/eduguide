# 2. University Overview

## History

The Royal University of Phnom Penh (RUPP) was established on **13 January 1960** as the **Royal Khmer University**, Cambodia's first national university. Throughout its history, the university underwent several name changes and institutional reforms following major political events in Cambodia. After reopening in the 1980s, RUPP gradually expanded its academic offerings and research capacity. In 1996, it officially became the Royal University of Phnom Penh (RUPP). Today it is Cambodia's oldest and largest public university.

https://www.rupp.edu.kh/index.php/about
