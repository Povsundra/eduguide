# 01. Vision

## Vision Statement

EduGuide aims to become Cambodia's trusted AI-powered educational advisor, empowering every student to make informed, personalized, and evidence-based higher education decisions through trustworthy artificial intelligence.

Rather than acting as a simple chatbot, EduGuide serves as an intelligent decision support system that combines structured educational knowledge, explainable reasoning, and grounded AI responses to guide students throughout their educational journey.

---

# Mission

EduGuide exists to reduce the information gap between Cambodian students and higher education opportunities.

The platform transforms fragmented educational information into personalized, explainable, and trustworthy guidance by integrating Knowledge Graph reasoning, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Agentic AI.

Our mission is to ensure that every student—regardless of location, socioeconomic background, or educational resources—can confidently explore universities, academic programs, scholarships, and career pathways using verified information from official sources.

---

# The Problem We Want to Solve

Choosing a university or academic program is one of the most important decisions in a student's life. However, educational information in Cambodia is currently scattered across numerous university websites, government portals, PDF documents, and social media pages.

Students often face challenges such as:

- Difficulty comparing universities and academic programs.
- Limited awareness of available scholarships.
- Unclear relationships between majors, skills, and career opportunities.
- Information overload from multiple disconnected sources.
- Lack of personalized educational guidance.
- Dependence on informal advice rather than evidence-based recommendations.

As a result, many students make educational decisions without sufficient understanding of available opportunities or future career outcomes.

EduGuide aims to transform this process by providing a single intelligent platform capable of understanding student needs, reasoning over educational knowledge, retrieving supporting evidence, and generating personalized recommendations.

---

# Product Vision

EduGuide is not a traditional chatbot.

EduGuide is an AI-powered Educational Decision Support System that combines multiple intelligent components to provide reliable educational guidance.

The platform assists students throughout the complete educational decision-making process by integrating:

- Student understanding through Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Rule-based filtering for hard constraints
- Knowledge Graph reasoning for recommendation generation
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) for evidence retrieval
- Grounded natural language explanations generated by LLMs

Rather than replacing human educational advisors, EduGuide is designed to augment and support decision-making through transparent and explainable artificial intelligence.

---

# Research Vision

This research investigates how Knowledge Graphs, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Agentic AI can be integrated into a unified educational decision support system.

Unlike conventional conversational AI systems that rely primarily on generative models, EduGuide separates reasoning, retrieval, and language generation into specialized components.

The research emphasizes:

- Explainable recommendation generation
- Evidence-based AI responses
- Transparent reasoning processes
- Trustworthy educational guidance
- Modular AI architecture
- Reproducible recommendation methods

The project demonstrates that combining symbolic reasoning with modern language models can produce more reliable and interpretable educational recommendations than using large language models alone.

# Design Philosophy

Every component of EduGuide should follow a consistent set of design principles.

## Student-Centered

Every design decision should prioritize helping students make better educational decisions.

---

## Evidence-First

Every recommendation should be supported by verified educational information retrieved from official sources.

---

## Explainability

Recommendations should always include understandable reasoning and supporting evidence rather than acting as a black-box AI system.

---

## Trustworthiness

The system must never fabricate educational information.

When sufficient evidence is unavailable, EduGuide should clearly communicate the limitation instead of generating unsupported responses.

---

## Graph-Driven Reasoning

Educational recommendations should be generated through Knowledge Graph reasoning and rule-based decision making rather than by Large Language Models.

---

## AI-Assisted Communication

Large Language Models should focus on:

- Understanding user intent.
- Extracting structured student profiles.
- Explaining recommendations naturally.
- Generating conversational responses.

LLMs should not determine educational recommendations independently.

---

## Modularity

Each intelligent component should have a clearly defined responsibility and be independently replaceable without affecting the entire system.

---

## Scalability

The architecture should support future expansion into additional educational services without requiring major redesign.

---

# Success Metrics

EduGuide achieves its vision when:

- Students can confidently identify suitable universities and academic programs.
- Recommendations are transparent, explainable, and evidence-based.
- Scholarship opportunities become easier to discover.
- Educational information becomes more accessible.
- Students spend less time searching for information and more time making informed decisions.
- Every recommendation can be traced back to verified knowledge sources.
- Users trust the system because it communicates both what it knows and what it does not know.

Success is measured not only by recommendation accuracy but also by transparency, explainability, user confidence, and trustworthiness.
