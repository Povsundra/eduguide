# 03.5 Domain Model

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Project:** EduGuide – An Agentic Retrieval-Augmented Decision Support System for Personalized Higher Education Guidance in Cambodia

---

# 1. Purpose

The Domain Model defines the conceptual structure of the EduGuide knowledge domain before it is formalized into an ontology or implemented as a Knowledge Graph.

Its primary objective is to identify the real-world entities, their meanings, ownership, relationships, and business rules that govern Cambodia's higher education ecosystem.

This document serves as the bridge between domain understanding and technical implementation. It ensures that all future system components—including the ontology, Knowledge Graph, recommendation engine, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and AI agents—share a consistent understanding of the educational domain.

This document intentionally remains independent of implementation technologies such as Neo4j, RDF, OWL, or database schemas.

---

# 2. Scope

Version 1 of the EduGuide Domain Model focuses exclusively on the minimum knowledge required to support personalized higher education recommendations.

Included entities are:

- University
- Program
- Curriculum
- Scholarship
- Admission Requirement
- Career
- Funder

The model intentionally excludes entities such as Faculty, Subject, Skill, Degree, Industry, and Internship, which are considered future extensions.

---

# 3. Design Principles

The EduGuide Domain Model follows several guiding principles.

## Real-World Representation

Every entity represents a real-world educational concept rather than a database object.

---

## Simplicity

Only concepts necessary for Version 1 are included.

Additional concepts will be introduced in future ontology versions.

---

## Reusability

Entities should be reusable whenever possible.

For example, the admission requirement "High School Diploma" may be shared by multiple academic programs and scholarships.

---

## Independence

Entities should exist independently whenever they have their own identity.

For example, scholarships exist independently of universities because they may be funded by governments, NGOs, industries, or universities.

---

## Explainability

Every relationship should represent a meaningful educational relationship that can be explained to end users.

---

# 4. Core Domain Entities

## 4.1 University

### Definition

A recognized higher education institution that offers one or more academic programs.

### Purpose

Universities are the primary educational providers within the EduGuide ecosystem.

### Examples

- Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC)
- Royal University of Phnom Penh (RUPP)
- Cambodia Academy of Digital Technology (CADT)
- American University of Phnom Penh (AUPP)

---

## 4.2 Program

### Definition

A structured academic degree or educational program offered by a university.

Programs represent the primary recommendation target of EduGuide.

### Examples

- Bachelor of Engineering in Data Science
- Bachelor of Computer Science
- Bachelor of Civil Engineering

---

## 4.3 Curriculum

### Definition

The official structure and sequence of courses that define an academic program.

A curriculum provides detailed information about learning outcomes and course organization.

Curricula have meaning only within the context of a specific academic program.

---

## 4.4 Scholarship

### Definition

A financial assistance opportunity that supports eligible students in pursuing higher education.

Scholarships may be provided by universities, governments, NGOs, industries, or international organizations.

Scholarships are modeled as independent entities because they may apply across multiple universities or academic programs.

---

## 4.5 Admission Requirement

### Definition

A requirement that applicants must satisfy before admission to an academic program or eligibility for a scholarship.

Admission requirements are reusable across multiple entities.

Examples include:

- High School Diploma
- Minimum GPA
- Recommendation Letter
- English Language Certificate

---

## 4.6 Career

### Definition

A professional occupation or career pathway commonly associated with graduates of an academic program.

Career entities represent expected employment outcomes rather than guaranteed job placements.

Examples

- Data Scientist
- Software Engineer
- Civil Engineer
- Network Administrator

---

## 4.7 Funder

### Definition

An organization responsible for financing or sponsoring scholarships.

Funders may include:

- Government agencies
- Universities
- Non-governmental organizations
- International organizations
- Industry partners

---

# 5. Ownership Decisions

Ownership defines whether an entity depends on another entity for its existence.

## University

Independent entity.

---

## Program

Owned by a University.

A program cannot exist without being offered by a university.

---

## Curriculum

Owned by a Program.

A curriculum has no independent meaning outside its associated academic program.

---

## Scholarship

Independent entity.

Scholarships are not owned by universities or programs.

Instead, they are connected through relationships indicating where they are available and who provides funding.

---

## Admission Requirement

Independent reusable entity.

The same requirement may apply to multiple programs and scholarships.

---

## Career

Independent entity.

Careers exist independently of educational institutions.

---

## Funder

Independent entity.

A funder may finance multiple scholarships.

---

# 6. Domain Relationships

## OFFERS

University → Program

Meaning

A university officially provides an academic program.

Cardinality

One University → Many Programs

---

## HAS_CURRICULUM

Program → Curriculum

Meaning

An academic program has one or more curriculum versions.

Cardinality

One Program → One or More Curricula

---

## HAS_ADMISSION_REQUIREMENT

Program → Admission Requirement

Meaning

Applicants must satisfy these requirements before entering the program.

Cardinality

Many Programs ↔ Many Admission Requirements

---

## AVAILABLE_AT

Scholarship → University

Meaning

The scholarship can be used at a particular university.

Cardinality

Many Scholarships ↔ Many Universities

---

## TARGETS

Scholarship → Program

Meaning

The scholarship specifically supports students enrolled in certain academic programs.

Cardinality

Many Scholarships ↔ Many Programs

---

## FUNDED_BY

Scholarship → Funder

Meaning

Identifies the organization responsible for financing the scholarship.

Cardinality

Many Scholarships → One Funder

---

## HAS_REQUIREMENT

Scholarship → Admission Requirement

Meaning

Applicants must satisfy these conditions before receiving the scholarship.

Cardinality

Many Scholarships ↔ Many Admission Requirements

---

## LEADS_TO

Program → Career

Meaning

Graduates from the program commonly pursue these careers.

This relationship represents common career pathways rather than guaranteed employment.

Cardinality

Many Programs ↔ Many Careers

# 7. Cardinality Summary

| Relationship | Cardinality |
|--------------|-------------|
| University → Program | 1 : N |
| Program → Curriculum | 1 : N |
| Program → Admission Requirement | N : N |
| Scholarship → University | N : N |
| Scholarship → Program | N : N |
| Scholarship → Funder | N : 1 |
| Scholarship → Admission Requirement | N : N |
| Program → Career | N : N |

---

# 8. Domain Constraints

The following rules define the behavior of the educational domain.

- Every Program must be offered by exactly one University.
- Every Curriculum belongs to one Program.
- Every Scholarship must have one Funder.
- A Scholarship may support multiple Programs.
- A Scholarship may be available at multiple Universities.
- Admission Requirements are reusable across Programs and Scholarships.
- A Program may prepare students for multiple Careers.
- A Career may be associated with multiple Programs.

---

The following examples demonstrate that the domain model supports the primary use cases of EduGuide.

### Q1

What programs does ITC offer?

University → OFFERS → Program

---

### Q2

Which universities offer Data Science?

---

### Q3

What scholarships are available for Data Science?

Program ← TARGETS ← Scholarship

---

### Q4

Which scholarships are available at ITC?

University ← AVAILABLE_AT ← Scholarship

---

### Q5

Who funds this scholarship?

Scholarship → FUNDED_BY → Funder

---

### Q6

What admission requirements are required for this program?

Program → HAS_ADMISSION_REQUIREMENT → Admission Requirement

---

### Q7

Does this scholarship require a recommendation letter?

Scholarship → HAS_REQUIREMENT → Admission Requirement

---

### Q8

What careers can I pursue after completing this program?

Program → LEADS_TO → Career

---

### Q9

Compare scholarships available for Computer Science at RUPP.

University ← AVAILABLE_AT ← Scholarship → TARGETS → Program

---

### Q10

Which government-funded scholarships are available for engineering students?

Funder → Scholarship → Program

# 10. Future Extensions

The current domain model intentionally limits its scope to the entities required for Version 1.

Future versions may introduce:

- Faculty
- Subject
- Skill
- Degree
- Industry
- Occupation
- Internship
- Research Area
- Learning Outcome
- Student Organization
- Exchange Program

The model has been designed to accommodate these additions without requiring significant structural changes.

---

# 11. Summary

The EduGuide Domain Model establishes a shared conceptual understanding of Cambodia's higher education ecosystem.

It defines the core educational entities, their meanings, ownership, relationships, and constraints while remaining independent of any implementation technology.

This model provides the conceptual foundation for the subsequent Ontology (Step 04), Knowledge Graph Design (Step 05), Recommendation Engine (Step 06), and Retrieval-Augmented Generation architecture (Step 07), ensuring consistency across all components of the EduGuide platform.
